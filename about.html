About us

Borrower-first loan tools for clearer decisions.

Docked helps Australian borrowers understand their likely loan position before they speak with a lender or broker. The site brings together simple calculators, plain-English loan answers, and an affordability guide that can help you prepare for the next conversation.

Docked is operated by Ginty United Investments. Our role is to provide general information, educational calculators, and enquiry routing. We are not a lender, mortgage broker, credit representative, or holder of an Australian credit licence.

What Docked does

  • Helps borrowers estimate repayments, borrowing power, LVR, refinance savings, and extra repayment impact.
  • Provides a bank statement quick check that runs in your browser where possible.
  • Lets borrowers request a follow-up from a licensed broker, lender, aggregator, or authorised credit representative.
  • Publishes practical borrower education in plain English.

How referrals work

If you submit an enquiry and give consent, Docked may share your details and affordability summary with a licensed broker, lender, aggregator, or authorised credit representative. Docked may receive a referral fee, affiliate commission, or similar benefit if your details are referred or if you proceed with a provider.

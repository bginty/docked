Privacy

Simple, respectful handling of your information.

This notice explains the information associated with the Docked website, PayPal-hosted checkout and customer support.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Who we are

Docked is the public brand operated by Ginty United Investments Pty Ltd. Privacy enquiries can be sent to support@docked.com.au.

This website

This static website does not provide a customer account, shopping cart or payment form. It does not collect card information. We do not ask you to enter payment details on docked.com.au.

The website host may process standard technical information, such as an IP address, browser details and request logs, to deliver and secure the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

PayPal-hosted checkout

Payment and delivery details are entered and handled through PayPal’s hosted checkout. PayPal’s own terms and privacy policies apply to that service. Docked does not receive your full card details.

Order and support information

Information received through PayPal or provided by you may include your name, contact details, delivery details, transaction details and messages. We use it to process, deliver and support an order, respond to enquiries, manage returns, keep appropriate business records and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably needed to process payment, deliver an order, provide support, operate the business or comply with law. We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for those purposes and applicable obligations.

Your choices and enquiries

You may ask a privacy question or request access to or correction of information held about you. Email support@docked.com.au. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

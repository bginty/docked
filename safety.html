Read before use

Safety comes before the swim.

This page is a concise safety overview. Always read and follow the final manual, markings, limits and warnings supplied with the product.

WARNING — ADULT RECREATIONAL POOL PRODUCT

For adults aged 18 years and over.

This product is not a life-saving device, personal flotation device or rescue device. It is intended for competent adult swimmers in calm, controlled swimming pools only.

Never use alone. Keep another responsible adult nearby.

Do not use in oceans, rivers, lakes, moving water, waves or currents.

Switch the propulsion system off before entering, exiting, lifting, inspecting or handling the product.

Keep hair, fingers, hands, feet, jewellery, swimwear ties and loose clothing away from propellers, guards, water intakes and moving components.

Never charge the product in or near water. Ensure all relevant components and connections are dry before charging.

Do not use a damaged, leaking, swollen, overheated or abnormally operating product, battery or charger.

Use only the approved battery and charger supplied or specified for the product.

Follow all instructions, occupancy limits, load limits and warnings supplied with the product.

Before every use

  • Read the final product manual and ensure you understand the controls.
  • Inspect the lounger and every relevant component for damage, unusual heat, swelling, leaks or abnormal operation.
  • Make sure all relevant closures and connections are correctly secured.
  • Check that the pool is calm, controlled and free of obstructions.
  • Confirm another responsible adult is nearby.

Propulsion and moving parts

Keep people, pool edges, steps, fittings and loose objects clear of moving components. Switch the propulsion system off before anyone approaches a propulsion area or before the product is handled.

Charging and storage

Charging must take place in a dry, suitable area away from the pool and other water. Do not charge a wet, damaged, swollen, leaking, overheated or abnormal battery. Follow the approved instructions for charging, storage and disposal.

If something seems wrong

Stop using the product immediately, move away from the water where it is safe to do so, and do not resume use until the issue has been assessed. Email support@docked.com.au for support.

Important: This website summary is not a substitute for the product manual or product-specific warnings supplied with the product. Follow the instructions, labels and limits provided for the delivered unit.

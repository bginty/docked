# Deals and sponsors — disabled

`DEALS_ENABLED=false` and `AFFILIATES_ENABLED=false`. Runtime configuration rejects true values; every stored deal is DRAFT_DISABLED and append-only. No offer, tracking redirect, affiliate commission or external campaign is activated.

The scaffold supports sports merchandise, media, tickets, Docked benefits, non-gambling sponsors and a separate BETTING_REVIEW_REQUIRED classification. Records contain sponsor, description, dates, jurisdiction, membership eligibility, terms, disclosure, tracking class, actor, reason and audit evidence. Tracking can only be NONE or a future consented aggregate design. No sportsbook credentials, cash stake, deposit data or customer losses enter this model.

Owner/admin with MFA may save a reviewed draft; auditors may inspect. A draft does not imply commercial or legal approval. Betting-related material requires its own current jurisdiction and platform review before a separate implementation can activate it. Missing/expired legal approval is not inferred from a sponsor contract or data licence.

Sponsors cannot buy selections, price/probability changes, standard-price verification, rank, favourable settlement, hidden losses or delayed Free safety data. These tables have no mutation path into either official or community performance. A later delivery integration must separately prove consent, clear advertising disclosure, complaint/unsubscribe handling and responsible-design controls.

# Adult-only merchandising policy

Status: **MANDATORY FOR ALL DOCKED AQUATIC PRODUCTS**  
Last updated: 13 August 2026

## Purpose and meaning

Docked is an adult pool leisure brand. Every aquatic product must be designed and marketed for people aged 18 years and over. In this policy, “adult” means the intended customer and user age; it does not mean sexual, restricted-goods or adult-content merchandising.

An 18+ label is a merchandising control, not a waiver and not evidence that a product falls outside an Australian mandatory standard or other safety obligation. Product design, foreseeable use, packaging, instructions and evidence still require SKU-specific classification and approval.

## Required presentation

Use clear, factual language such as:

- Adult pool leisure
- Adults 18+ only
- Designed for adult pool relaxation
- Premium pool lounging for adults
- Adult pool entertaining
- For controlled swimming-pool use

Display a prominent but tasteful **Adults 18+** badge on:

- powered-float product cards and product pages;
- adult-lounger product pages;
- adult-pool-game product pages;
- the Safety and Care page; and
- powered-float comparison sections.

Place the product-specific safety summary near Add to Cart. Important warnings must not be confined to a collapsed panel or reduced to inconspicuous text.

## Prohibited audience language

Do not use or target language such as:

- for kids, children or teens;
- for the whole family, family fun or all ages;
- children’s pool toy or kids’ ride-on;
- baby float; or
- learn-to-swim product.

Do not target “kids pool toys”, “children’s pool floats”, “baby pool float”, “toddler pool toys” or “children’s swim aid” in visible copy, metadata, search synonyms or advertising. American spelling may support genuine adult-focused search terms, but visible copy should use Australian spelling.

## Image and media policy

- Do not show children or teenagers in product photos, demonstrations, lifestyle imagery, social-sharing images, advertising, displayed user-generated content, Docked-created instructions, review imagery or banners.
- Human models must clearly appear to be adults.
- Before publication, confirm the media depicts the exact approved SKU and safe, controlled pool use consistent with its manual and warnings.
- Record the source and licence for every third-party asset. Supplier media requires documented permission.
- Do not use competitor imagery, scraped marketplace media, fake product renders as sales imagery or imagery that obscures required warnings.
- Until approved product photography exists, use only clearly labelled original abstract/vector preview artwork and keep products Draft.

## Products outside the range

Do not create Active listings for baby or infant floats/seats, children’s armbands or flotation aids, mermaid tails, children’s ride-ons or novelty inflatables, paddling/portable pools, children’s slides/noodles, learn-to-swim aids, life jackets, personal flotation devices, child-targeted diving toys, or products primarily designed or commonly recognised as children’s aquatic toys.

Also exclude products whose supplier packaging conflicts with the adult-only position or whose supplier, safety, testing or traceability information cannot be verified. Never alter, cover or remove a legally required supplier warning.

## Age-confirmation control

The optional theme age-confirmation feature must remain disabled by default. It may be enabled only after legal or insurance advice and owner approval. It must not be presented as a release of liability or a restriction of Australian Consumer Law rights, and it does not replace badges, warnings, classification or safe-product evidence.

## SKU approval gate

Before a product can become Active, review its design, size, capacity, foreseeable use, supplier marketing, final packaging, instructions, imagery, age grading and applicable electrical/battery features. Confirm that all customer-facing copy and media match the approved product and evidence. Calling a product “adult only” must never be used to avoid a safety standard.

Use the [Adult-only content audit](ADULT_ONLY_CONTENT_AUDIT.md) before every release and whenever content, media, supplier packaging, navigation, search configuration or a product feed changes.

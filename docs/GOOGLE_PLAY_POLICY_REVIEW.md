# Docked Preview — Google Play policy review

Reviewed 3 October 2026 against the official pages linked below. **Draft assessment, not legal advice, Google approval, a submitted declaration, or authority to release.** Closed testing is the intended track. Production, affiliates, advertising, paid plans, prizes and competitions remain off.

## Product facts to disclose

Docked contains sports-market analysis, community opinions, reference prices, permanent member Edge records and performance architecture. It does not take wagers, hold funds, place bets, create sportsbook accounts or provide a wallet. Future paid analysis exists in the architecture but is disabled. The beta has no configured odds/results provider, no validated strategy and no live official selections. Synthetic Preview prices are visibly labelled and physically isolated from genuine accounting. Reviewers must see those actual features, including the preview access policy; do not hide them for review.

## Gambling policy — decision required

Google regulates apps that enable or facilitate gambling, not only apps holding money. Licensed gambling apps have a separate application and territory/age requirements. Its gambling-ad section specifically restricts combining gambling ads with odds/performance companion functionality. That restriction belongs to the **advertising section**; it is not a blanket statement that all sports discussion or odds displays are prohibited. Docked currently has no such ads. [Official policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032?hl=en)

**Project assessment:** Docked’s actionable TAKE terminology, reference-price comparisons and community betting opinions need legal and Play classification review. Absence of wagering does not establish eligibility. Keep all bookmaker deal/affiliate calls to action disabled. Do not disguise functionality or relabel wagering assistance to evade review. Owner should supply the complete feature inventory to counsel and, where appropriate, seek clarification from Play before selecting countries or submitting. No Australian state has been newly approved by this milestone.

## Age, audience and territories

The intended beta audience is adults. Self-attestation is not verified age or identity. Google’s age-restricted functionality policy requires Play tools to block minors for covered categories, including gambling facilitation; whether that category applies requires the classification above. Choose the actual target audience and complete the IARC questionnaire honestly; do not predeclare an awarded rating. [Age restrictions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16302250?hl=en), [target audience](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en), [content ratings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655?hl=en)

Country selection remains **OWNER / LEGAL CONFIRMATION REQUIRED**. Proposed initial scope is invited adult testers in the owner’s reviewed location only; Australia is not selected or approved automatically. Play country availability is not a state-level jurisdiction control. Exact-account Preview permission is a temporary testing entitlement, not a production legal permission.

## Community safety and operational ownership

Google requires accepted terms, defined prohibited behaviour and ongoing effective moderation, with in-app reporting/blocking for relevant UGC. Docked implements terms acceptance, reports, blocks, mutes and moderator controls. Having controls is not evidence of an operating moderation service. Owner must name the moderator, establish response times/escalation and verify the final terms before inviting members. No anonymous random matching or private-message service is introduced. [UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en)

## Privacy, deletion and review access

Data Safety must describe actual app and SDK collection across the distributed version; closed testing is not the internal-testing exemption. Draft inventory is in [the closed-beta listing pack](GOOGLE_PLAY_CLOSED_BETA.md). Account creation also requires discoverable in-app deletion and an external deletion request resource; the final public URL and contact owner need verification. Explain necessary retained pseudonymous audit/immutable records, their purpose and retention period. Never claim that all records vanish immediately when only personal identifiers are erased. [Data Safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469), [account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)

Play reviewers need working access to restricted areas. Supply a separate revocable review account privately through Console, with instructions explaining demo records and closed gates. Do not give the retained S24 account, admin credentials or short-lived QA passwords as a permanent reviewer login. [Testing and review access](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465)

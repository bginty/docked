# Docked compliance register

Status: **PRELAUNCH — ALL PRODUCT RECORDS BLOCKED**  
Register date: 13 August 2026

Website completion does not certify a physical product. This register records evidence and decisions for the exact products Docked proposes to supply. No final supplier, manufacturer, model, SKU, barcode, production sample, report or approval has been received, and no product may be Active.

## Register controls

Every requirement record uses the fields required by the project brief:

- **Requirement**
- **Applicable/not applicable**
- **Evidence required**
- **Evidence received**
- **Document location**
- **Reviewed by**
- **Review date**
- **Expiry/retest date**
- **Status**
- **Notes**

Permitted statuses are `Not assessed`, `Pending evidence`, `Under review`, `Approved`, `Not applicable — rationale approved`, `Expired` and `Rejected`. “Not applicable” is not a default and needs a written rationale approved for the exact SKU. An owner instruction, supplier declaration or overseas compliance mark alone does not establish Australian compliance.

`Concept ID` is an internal register key only; it is not an invented commercial SKU.

## Change control

Any change to supplier, manufacturer, factory, model, component, cell, battery pack, charger, controller, firmware, material, colourway, valve, guard, packaging, label, manual, claim or intended use reopens all affected requirements. Only an authorised reviewer may mark a record `Approved` or `Not applicable — rationale approved`, and the reviewer must add a dated evidence reference.

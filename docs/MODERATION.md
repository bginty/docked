# Community moderation — preview operating rules

Prepared 3 October 2026. Member discussion is separate from verified pricing and permanent performance evidence.

Members can report spam, harassment, impersonation, privacy breaches, scams, affiliate spam, misleading odds and other concerns. A report targets one post, comment or profile; duplicate reports by the same reporter against the same target do not inflate the queue. Reporter identity is private. Reports do not automatically erase records or establish guilt.

Owner/admin/editor with MFA may remove disallowed commentary, warn, restrict posting, suspend, restore an eligible profile, approve/reject quarantined media, dismiss a report or escalate it. Auditors may inspect the queue and protected media with MFA but cannot write. Actions record actor, target, reason, timestamp and report linkage in immutable moderation events. Official account ownership cannot be reassigned through moderation. A restricted member retains privacy controls and reporting but cannot post/react/follow; a suspended account cannot use member social surfaces.

Removing a social post or comment clears its body. Associated Edge commentary displays a removal notice; canonical market, price, verification, time, correction, settlement and performance records remain intact. Moderators cannot remove losing records from a verified denominator. Staff may not approve a screenshot as provider odds/results evidence. Promotional text remains labeled social-only or promotional and cannot become verified by changing a label.

Uploads are never public while quarantined. The admin preview route requires a verified active staff session and MFA; privileged case review works before public community-region activation. Ordinary member image access still requires current region approval and an eligible social account. Approved attachments require matching ownership. Rejection erases stored image bytes. Moderators should inspect for privacy, abusive content, malware indicators, misleading verification claims, unauthorized branding and rights issues. Automated malware scanning is not configured in this preview and must be resolved before an unrestricted production upload launch.

Rate limits apply to profile changes, posting, replies, reports, media and requests. Blocks remove reciprocal follows and pending inbox entries between the accounts; mutes suppress feed discovery and new notifications. Blocked or private commentary is hidden, while canonical performance remains accessible with appropriate identity redaction.

Operational legal decisions remain open: jurisdiction-specific community approval, response and escalation SLAs, appeal handling, evidence retention schedules and staff training. The current audited `restore` action can implement a reviewed appeal; no automated appeals service is claimed.

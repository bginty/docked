## Exact-product photography requirement

No exact-production-SKU documentary image is currently approved. Before publishing any documentary product photograph, obtain and record:

- the exact supplied SKU/model shown from multiple angles;
- photographer or supplier identity and written commercial-use permission;
- permitted channels, territory, term, editing rights and restrictions;
- adult-only and safety review, with no contradictory operating context;
- confirmation that every visible component and accessory matches the delivered product.

Until exact-product photography is approved, the public site uses only original, non-depictive brand artwork and clearly states that the gallery is illustrative rather than documentary product photography. No supplied reference image is published as a depiction of the delivered product.

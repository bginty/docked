## Accounting

One standard unit per official Edge. Published count includes pending/void records. Settled count includes wins/losses/voids; pending remains separate. A win earns publication odds minus one, a loss minus one, a void zero. ROI's settled non-void unit denominator is displayed/documented; no-stake/no-settlement ROI is unavailable rather than zero. Average published odds and estimated Edge describe all qualifying published records, including pending/voids; maximum drawdown and longest losing run use ordered actual settlements and transparent corrections. No average or rate is fabricated before observations exist.

The stored publication binds the independent probability, fair price, upward-rounded minimum, publication Market Reference, estimated EV, model/input/config/code versions, data cutoff, sporting and market provenance, current approving actor and server publication timestamp. Corrections append; original prices/probabilities do not change when the market moves or a selection loses.

Current market at/above minimum may be ACTIVE only while data/event/rights gates remain valid. Below minimum means EDGE GONE / PRICE BELOW MINIMUM, with the original publication retained. Unavailable/stale data does not imply a zero price or settle an event. Existing conservative non-reactivation controls remain.

Only a current authorised admin can approve/reject a candidate. Approval revalidates model/version/data, current Market Reference, EV/minimum, event, strategy, jurisdiction and provider health. No editable official probability/Fair/reference fields are exposed. Auto-publication remains false.

The new philosophy supersedes earlier historical betting ROI requirements in [Strategy V1](STRATEGY_V1.md), [validation](VALIDATION.md) and research planning. Historical sporting data and chronological model/software tests remain useful; they are not official tips or public simulated profit.

This is not a definitive legal classification or blanket clearance. Keep wagering, bookmaker promotions, trading, paid/prize features and unvalidated official recommendations disabled. Any change needs a fresh applicability review. Preserve non-excludable consumer rights. [ACMA](https://www.acma.gov.au/about-interactive-gambling-act), [ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/contracts).

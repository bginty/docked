## Current Australian guidance checked

- [OAIC small business](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business): coverage depends on turnover and exceptions; do not assume exemption. Owner must supply the relevant business facts privately.
- [OAIC de-identification](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/handling-personal-information/de-identification-and-the-privacy-act): where APP 11.2 applies, unnecessary personal information must be destroyed or de-identified, subject to exceptions. A retained account UUID is not automatically anonymous; permanent scarcity is not a blanket retention basis.
- [ACCC contracts](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/contracts): terms cannot simply exclude applicable consumer rights; assess unfair unilateral changes and suspension terms. Describe actual availability and beta limitations accurately.
- [ACMA Interactive Gambling Act](https://www.acma.gov.au/about-interactive-gambling-act): online gambling provision and advertising have specific restrictions. Free entry/no cash reduces some concerns but does not itself settle classification of all game, promotion and social features. Keep paid transactions, prizes, bookmaker promotion and unvalidated publication disabled.
- [eSafety codes](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes) and [service categories](https://www.esafety.gov.au/industry/codes/faq-access-to-online-porn-and-other-adult-content): online games and communication services can fall within codes/standards. Assess actual posting, image and chat functionality rather than relying on a “beta” label or age checkbox. [Minimum-age guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/about-us/industry-regulation/social-media-age-restrictions) requires assessing the service's actual classification.
- [Gambling Help Online](https://www.gamblinghelponline.org.au/): Australian support information and 1800 858 858 verified. No affiliation claimed.

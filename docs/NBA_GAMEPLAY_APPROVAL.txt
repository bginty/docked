A. TEAM FORMAT — APPROVED FOR PROTECTED TESTING; PROPOSED FOR LIVE USE
Eight unique players: PG, SG, SF, PF, C, Guard (PG/SG), Forward (SF/PF), Utility (any of those five positions).
Only reviewed roster positions count. Different serials or rarities cannot duplicate a player. Ownership and period eligibility remain mandatory.
Salary Cap and Open have separate teams. Existing first-team Auto Select, manual substitutions, saved drafts, rollover and historical snapshots are reused. Rollover copies selections into the next draft; it does not silently submit an entry.
Auto Select uses the existing exact optimiser: fill the valid formation, favour known rankings, maximise their sum, then prefer lower salary and stable IDs. In the isolated NBA laboratory, rankings use up to five prior scored games, excluding DNP and the current week. No history means explicitly unranked, not an invented projection. A saved draft or established team cannot be overwritten by Auto Select.
The existing fixed-name rule is retained. The simulation engine also rejects renaming an established NBA proposal entry.

B. SCORING — APPROVED FOR PROTECTED TESTING; PROPOSED FOR LIVE USE
One authoritative scorer, integer hundredths of a fantasy point:
Point scored        +1.00   SportsDataIO Points -> points
Rebound             +1.20   Rebounds -> rebounds
Assist              +1.50   Assists -> assists
Steal               +3.00   Steals -> steals
Block               +3.00   BlockedShots -> blocks
Turnover            -1.00   Turnovers -> turnovers
Double-double       +1.50   Derived from the five positive categories above
Triple-double       +3.00   Additional to the double-double bonus

Ten or more in at least two qualifying categories earns the first bonus; at least three earns both. Bonuses are assessed per game, never from combined weekly statistics. No extra reward for a fourth qualifying category is introduced. Made threes are already included in Points; the existing transport retains ThreePointersMade with zero additional weight. Overtime counts.
Example: 28 points, 9 rebounds, 7 assists, 2 steals, 1 block, 3 turnovers = 55.30.
Example: 10 points, 10 rebounds, 10 assists = 10 + 12 + 15 + 1.5 + 3 = 41.50.
Negative scores are allowed. Confirmed zero performance displays 0.00. Missing data remains unresolved.
Version: docked-nba-weekly-proposal-v1. Earlier docked-nba-events-v1 and all saved NFL/EPL/AFL/NRL rules are unchanged. New weights are not a correction to old results.
NBA's category definitions: https://jr.nba.com/double-double-and-triple-double/
The scoring weights and stacking policy are Docked's owner proposal, not NBA or provider endorsement.

C. SALARY CAP — APPROVED FOR PROTECTED TESTING; PROPOSED FOR LIVE USE
Cap $5,200,000. Core $500,000; Rare $600,000; Elite $650,000; Ultra Rare $700,000; Legendary $800,000; Icon $800,000. Open has no cap.
These values are copied into an isolated NBA configuration, not the global economy.
Reproducible simulation: npx tsx scripts/simulate-nba-weekly.ts
All-Core: $4m. All-Rare: $4.8m. All-Elite: exactly $5.2m. All-Ultra Rare: $5.6m, invalid under the cap. All-Legendary/Icon: $6.4m, invalid. Four Core plus four Icon: exactly $5.2m.
In 1,000 clearly synthetic 25-card collections, the cap changed the optimum in 694. Mean capped score 1,405.5906 versus 1,460.56 Open. These are arithmetic stress scenarios, not forecasts of real player performance or member collections.
Critical trade-off: two cards of the same player score identically. The Core copy costs less salary, so it is at least as useful for fantasy selection. Rarity value comes from scarcity, collectibility, ownership and trading, not extra NBA points.
Recommendation: retain the proposal for an isolated owner playtest, but explicitly accept this trade-off before launch. Do not promise a fantasy advantage for buying rarity. A performance-based salary model would be a separate business-rule change; none was implemented.

D. WEEKLY COMPETITIONS — APPROVED FOR PROTECTED TESTING; PROPOSED FOR LIVE USE
Monday 00:00 to the following Monday 00:00 EXCLUSIVE in America/New_York (equivalent to all of Sunday, including its last fractional second). This avoids a one-minute gap from interpreting 23:59 literally.
Calendar arithmetic handles 167-hour spring and 169-hour autumn weeks. Explicit UTC/offset timestamps are required; ambiguous local timestamps are rejected.
Assign by scheduled start, not finish. A Sunday game finishing Monday stays in its starting week. Each stable fixture belongs to one frozen week; repeated imports do not add another game.
All eight slots lock at the first eligible game's scheduled start; edits at or after that instant are rejected. Times display in the viewer's local timezone. No rolling player locks.
Every completed eligible game contributes. Example 44.5 + 38.0 + 51.2 = 133.7. No best-game selection or per-game normalisation.
Four equal performances earn four times one performance. This creates a strong schedule-volume strategy; owner acceptance is required. No unapproved balancing adjustment was added.
Provisional rankings use known postgame points, with shared ranks for ties. Stable member-ID ordering only stabilises display and is NOT a prize tie-break rule. Approve any prize tie-break separately before awards are enabled.

E. DNP, EXCEPTIONS AND CORRECTIONS
Confirmed non-participation is zero for that game; it counts toward week completion, but not “played”. Injury, inactivity labels, missing rows, zero minutes or Games=0 alone do not establish DNP in the SportsDataIO adapter.
The existing canonical feed can carry confirmed DNP evidence. SportsDataIO's current adapter deliberately leaves unconfirmed participation unresolved. A documented provider participation/roster evidence route must be accepted and verified with real samples before activation.
Played and week-complete are different counters: a player can have played one game while awaiting three more. Scores remain provisional until required games and the correction window are resolved.
Postponed, cancelled, suspended, abandoned or rescheduled games require explicit reconciliation. They do not become fabricated DNP/zero games. No injury compensation or replacement points.
A trade never creates a new player ID. Games across both teams can aggregate once within the frozen window. If a trade or reschedule changes already-frozen player/fixture membership, the current system blocks silent remapping and requires reviewed reconciliation. The exact post-lock exceptional membership policy remains an activation decision; the laboratory does not silently rewrite snapshots.
Corrections replace the same game revision and recalculate scores/ranks, retaining audit history. Replays are idempotent. Existing 72-hour reviewer finalisation and protected historical-correction controls remain in force. No prize or wallet effects are added.

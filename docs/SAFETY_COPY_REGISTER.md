## SC-01 — universal adult safety notice

Applies to all aquatic products. Draft pending supplier, Australian product-safety and legal review.

> **WARNING — ADULT RECREATIONAL POOL PRODUCT, AGES 18+**  
> This product is not a life-saving device or personal flotation device. It is intended for competent adult swimmers in calm, controlled swimming pools only. Never use alone. Do not use in oceans, rivers, lakes, moving water, waves or currents. Follow the supplied instructions and all verified user, occupancy and weight limits. Inspect the product before every use and stop using it if it is damaged, leaking or operating abnormally.

Placement: show the warning heading and critical summary beside Add to Cart without requiring expansion. Show the complete paragraph in the product Safety section and on the Safety and Care page. Final product/packaging/manual placement requires exact-SKU approval.

## SC-02 — additional universal safety points

Use each point where relevant. Draft pending supplier, Australian product-safety and legal review.

- Adults aged 18 years and over only.
- Never rely on the product to prevent drowning.
- Use only with another responsible adult present.
- Do not use while impaired by alcohol, recreational drugs, sedating medication, illness, exhaustion or another condition that could affect judgement or swimming ability.
- Do not stand, dive, jump or perform stunts on the product.
- Do not tow the product behind a boat or other powered equipment.
- Do not use it as a rescue device, bridge, step, diving platform or pool-safety product.
- Stay clear of pool drains, suction outlets, skimmers, fountains and operating pool-cleaning equipment.
- Keep away from sharp objects, rough surfaces, glass and open flames.
- Follow the verified maximum user weight and occupancy.
- Check valves, seams, handles, motors and attachments before use.
- Stop using the product immediately if it loses air or stability.
- Enter and leave the product according to the supplied instructions.
- Store completely dry and away from excessive heat and direct sunlight when not in use.

Placement: complete set on Safety and Care; applicable set in each product Safety section; the exact-SKU review decides which critical items must also appear near Add to Cart, in Setup/Use/Care, and on the product/packaging/manual.

## SC-03 — powered-product warning

Applies to every powered-float page. Draft pending supplier, Australian product-safety and legal review.

> **POWERED PRODUCT WARNING**  
> Switch the propulsion system off before entering, exiting, lifting, inspecting or handling the float. Keep hair, fingers, hands, feet, jewellery, swimwear ties and loose clothing away from propellers, guards, water intakes and moving components. Never operate a damaged or obstructed motor. Use only the supplied or specifically approved battery and charger. Never charge the product in or near water. Ensure the product, battery compartment and charging connection are fully dry before charging.

Placement: display separately from SC-01 near Add to Cart, with full text in product Safety and Charging sections and on Safety and Care. The final exact-SKU review must align terminology such as “propeller”, “guard”, “water intake”, “battery compartment” and “charging connection” to the actual design.

## SC-04 — additional powered-product safety points

Use where relevant on powered products. Draft pending supplier, Australian product-safety and legal review.

- Do not operate the motor while another person is touching the propulsion unit.
- Do not remove a propeller guard while the system is powered.
- Do not attempt to clear an obstruction while the battery is connected.
- Do not operate near another swimmer’s hair, hands or clothing.
- Do not use where the pool is too small for safe turning and stopping.
- Do not use near pool covers, ropes, nets or loose equipment.
- Do not leave the battery charging unattended unless the approved manual expressly permits it.
- Stop charging and isolate the product if the battery becomes unusually hot, swollen, damaged, wet, emits an odour or behaves abnormally.
- Do not use an unapproved replacement charger or battery.
- Follow the supplier’s storage-charge requirements.
- Dispose of batteries through an approved battery-recycling service.
- Never describe the entire product as waterproof unless that exact claim is supported.
- Clearly identify which components are water resistant and their verified rating.

Placement: full applicable set in product Safety, Charging and Care sections and on Safety and Care; surface isolation, entanglement, dry charging and abnormal-battery actions near the purchase/use controls. Replacement battery/charger pages need their own review.

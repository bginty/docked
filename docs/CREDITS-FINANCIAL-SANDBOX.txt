FIXED COMMERCIAL RULES MODELLED IN THE SANDBOX
- A$1 = 100 Docked Credits; one credit corresponds to one AUD cent for calculations.
- A$49 verified deposit: 4,900 purchased credits PLUS one unopened Premium entitlement.
- A$99 verified deposit: 9,900 purchased credits PLUS one unopened Elite entitlement.
- Bonus consumes no credits. Additional Premium/Elite packs cost 4,900/9,900 credits;
  credit purchases do not grant another deposit bonus.
- Seller commission 7.5%; buyer pays the listing price, seller receives net and Docked
  receives fee in the same transaction as ownership transfer.
- Proposed whole-credit rounding: half-up, floor((price*750+5000)/10000), implemented
  with BigInt. Examples: price1→fee0; 6→0; 7→1; 20→2; 100→8; 4900→368; 9900→743.
- Pure swaps: exactly 50 credits each, 100 total, only on atomic completion. Cancel,
  reject, expire, insufficient funds, stale ownership and locked lineup failures charge
  nothing. Offers do not reserve funds/cards; acceptance rechecks under one DB lock.
- Mixed card-plus-credit trades are rejected by the command schema.
- Rarity, purchase price and spending do not enter any fantasy scoring calculation.

LEDGER, OWNERSHIP AND RECOVERY
Integer double-entry postings with immutable hash-linked journal, SQL sum constraint,
unique journal/request references and append-only snapshots. No balance-edit operation.
Available, pending and reserved buckets are disjoint. Spendable excludes review-held
funds; withdrawable is an eligible subset, never added to the wallet total.
Lots retain purchased/payment, sale, promotion and competition provenance. Transfers
preserve the source eligibility. Proposal only: promotional/bonus-derived cards remain
restricted on resale, including through intervening swaps, so unbacked credits cannot
be washed into redeemable liabilities. This conservative restriction requires approval
before live use and must be disclosed to both parties. It is not a friends-beta rule.
Corrections append compensating lines. Spent awards/refunds and transferred bonus cards
create explicit recovery cases; no third-party card confiscation or automatic debt.
Partial refunds are cumulative, monotonic and idempotent; multiple pending refunds
cannot leave duplicate credit. Unopened refunded bonus entitlements enter review until
the remedy policy is decided. No automatic bonus forfeiture was invented.
Funding identifiers/budgets gate redeemable promotional and competition awards. A
recorded sandbox funding entry is NOT proof of bank cash. Approved schedule version,
finalised eligible result, tie policy and result revision accompany each award.
Result corrections hold the existing obligation for review rather than pay again.
Same award identity with changed schedule/input is rejected. Prize values are examples.
KYC uses existing session/member/revision checks and no real documents. Withdrawal
request/reserve, reviewed, submitted, uncertain, paid, failed and cancelled transitions
are guarded. Uncertain submissions cannot be resubmitted or cancelled; only definitive
reconciliation releases or spends the reserve. Owner/admin operations require AAL2;
ordinary fictional member actions require no MFA. Actual auth integration is unchanged.

SUPPORTED FUNDS FLOW PROPOSAL (CONDITIONAL, NOT A CLAIM OF VERIFIED PROVIDER SUPPORT)
Prefer a provider specifically approved to hold/safeguard customer value and handle
marketplace beneficiaries/payouts. Docked records the matching subledger; member deposits
remain liabilities. Identify in writing who legally holds funds and is the facility
issuer, who contracts with sellers, who pays/absorbs processing fees, who manages reserves,

DECISIONS REQUIRED BEFORE LIVE FINANCIAL ACTIVATION — RECOMMENDATIONS ONLY
1. Bonus frequency: recommend one qualifying bonus per verified member per published,
   budgeted campaign, not unlimited replenishment. Choose the A$49/A$99 upgrade rule and
   campaign dates/cap. The lab uses one per product solely to exercise both paths.
2. Deposits later withdrawn: recommend no undisclosed spend requirement. Price the
   campaign assuming principal may be returned. Decide whether a properly earned bonus
   survives withdrawal/refund and the unopened-pack remedy; never seize a later buyer's
   card. Repeated deposit/withdraw loops otherwise drain bonus supply and processing funds.
3. Minimum withdrawal: propose A$10 (1,000 credits); test fixture uses A$1 to exercise
   boundaries. This is not approved. Payout fee: recommend zero initially only if Docked
   can fund actual rail costs; otherwise disclose/approve a cost-based fee before use.
4. Holds: release after verified provider settlement and disclosed risk review; determine
   provider-specific dispute/settlement periods with an appeal/review process. Do not
   claim zero-delay sandbox clearing is a safe live hold period.
5. Purchased credits: recommend redemption of eligible cleared principal without mandatory
   spend, subject to approved provider/refund routing and lawful disclosed risk controls.
6. Promotional spending: recommended non-withdrawable, campaign-limited lots and explicit
   spend order (lab: promotion, competition, sale, purchased). Approve resale restrictions
   including swap propagation, or fund eligible conversion from identified company cash.
   Lab restrictions are conservative demonstrations, not silently approved member terms.
7. Competition awards: choose amounts, schedule version, eligibility, ties, funding budget
   and redeemability. No values/automatic awards are approved by this implementation.
8. Whole-credit commission rounding: approve proposed half-up; decide if a minimum listing
   price is needed. Pure swaps stay exactly 50 each; mixed offers remain disabled.
9. Product supply: approve finite editions, eligibility pool/ranking source and availability
   remedy before offering guaranteed deposit bonuses. Tier/random choice is already recorded.
10. Provider activation: confirm exact-model written approval and securely configure a
    sandbox REST client secret, merchant ID, webhook ID and test buyers in local environment
    or approved secret storage. Do not paste secrets into chat. Confirm refund/dispute and
    payout permissions separately, then run real sandbox acceptance before any live proposal.

## Preliminary public-register findings

The following searches were performed against the public interfaces on 14 August 2026. They are a dated screening step only, not an ASIC extract, legal opinion or comprehensive trademark clearance.

- The [ASIC business-names register](https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/RegistrySearch/faces/landing/bn/SearchBnRegisters.jspx) exact search for **Docked** returned four business-name records. The exact **DOCKED** record displayed status **Registered**, registration date **9 September 2010**, and holder **DOMINIC PRONESTI**—not GINTY UNITED INVESTMENTS PTY LTD. The public page also displayed an historical renewal date; that display does not authorise this project to infer ownership, availability, expiry or a right to use the name.
- ASIC explains that business-name availability and trademark rights are separate checks. See [ASIC business-name availability tests](https://asic.gov.au/for-business/registering-a-business-name/before-you-register-a-business-name/business-name-availability/business-name-availability-tests/).
- The [IP Australia Trade Mark Search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick/result?q=Docked) exact quick search for **Docked** returned zero results. A broader quick search for **Dock** returned 170 results across multiple statuses and classes. Similar marks, spelling/phonetic variants and the intended goods/services have not been comprehensively reviewed.

The exact ASIC result is a production-launch blocker. It is not, by itself, a conclusion about infringement, trademark rights or what commercial resolution may be available.

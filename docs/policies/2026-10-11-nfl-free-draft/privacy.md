# Free NFL friends beta — privacy notice draft

Prepared 11 October 2026. **Not activated.** This carries forward the owner's approved support process and conditional retention schedule. Remaining effective mailbox retention/hold and provider-preservation facts must be reconciled before activation. It does not replace historical consent documents or assert legal review.

Docked is operated by Ginty United Investments Pty Ltd, ABN 78 606 187 106. Contact support@docked.com.au for support, moderation, access, correction or account-closure requests.

This beta is free, invitation-only, limited to ten Australian adults and initially supports NFL fantasy cards and post-game scoring. EPL is unavailable until its data and scoring are verified. Beta cards, points and swaps have no cash value. Payments, paid packs, cash trading, prizes, withdrawals and identity-document collection are disabled.

We use your account contact details, profile, age/country attestations, consent versions, security records, owned cards, teams, competitions, scoring records and community contributions to run and protect the beta. Publicly displayed profiles, posts and competition information follow the app's visibility and moderation controls. Ordinary members are not required to enrol in MFA; owner/admin access requires it. Do not send identity documents or payment-card details to support.

Supabase provides authentication and the database configured in Sydney; Vercel provides website delivery with Sydney functions. Microsoft Exchange Online provides the support mailbox and transactional email, purchased and administered through GoDaddy. GoDaddy also lists Advanced Email Security for the mailbox. Regional hosting does not establish all processing locations; we do not promise Australia-only processing. We are confirming the mailbox's at-rest location and effective preservation settings with the reseller. Security filtering, recovery, provider backups and applicable holds can preserve copies after active messages are removed.

The owner operates support and moderation, checks the inbox daily and aims to acknowledge routine requests within two business days and resolve routine privacy requests within 30 days. We verify that requests belong to the account without collecting identity documents through ordinary email. Urgent safety/security reports receive prompt containment and review. Contact emergency services for immediate danger. Suspected data breaches are escalated for specialist assessment where needed. We explain unresolved requests, necessary extensions and available review options.

On a verified closure request we revoke beta access immediately. Eligible account/contact details and published posts/media are scheduled for removal within 30 days. We retain the minimum ownership/game evidence needed for ongoing rounds or disputes, then remove account-to-game identity links within 30 days after those purposes close. Minimised evidence is reviewed at beta end and deleted or further de-identified within 12 months of beta closure unless a documented exception remains. We do not describe still-linkable records as anonymous.

Routine support cases are scheduled for removal 12 months after closure. Exported support working copies are removed after delivery verification, no later than seven days. Routine operational logs have a 30-day maximum target and security logs a 90-day maximum target; some provider logs expire sooner. Exceptional incident/dispute evidence requires a recorded reason and review at least every 90 days. These are operating commitments, not a claim that every provider has matching automatic deletion settings.

We can provide a support-assisted export of available account and gameplay records and selected support correspondence. Mailbox export has been demonstrated on a synthetic message; this does not prove permanent deletion from every provider copy. We explain known recovery, backup or hold limits when handling deletion requests and reapply completed deletions before restored data returns to active use. Immutable scarcity and scoring history do not justify retaining names and email addresses indefinitely.

Optional marketing remains disabled. No invitation is sent without the owner's explicit recipient authorisation. No historical storefront mailing list is imported into this beta.

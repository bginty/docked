# Docked privacy notice — owner review draft

Version: 2026-10-31-public-review. NOT APPROVED, NOT PUBLISHED, NOT USED FOR CONSENT.
Do not replace accepted beta policy versions with this file. Resolve the publication checklist below first.

Docked is operated by Ginty United Investments Pty Ltd, ABN 78 606 187 106. Contact support@docked.com.au about privacy, access, correction, deletion or a complaint.

## Information we handle

We handle your email address and account identifiers, login and security records, profile information, country/state and age attestations, accepted policy versions and preferences. Required account information supports authentication, eligibility, account recovery and the service. Optional profile details and community contributions are your choice.

We record cards and ownership transfers, permanent serial and supply records, packs, credit transactions, marketplace sales, fantasy teams, competition entries and results. These support your collection, accurate scoring, transaction reconciliation, dispute resolution and fraud prevention. NFL, AFL and NRL share one account. Test credits and test inventory are identified in the product; they do not become cash merely because a record is retained.

Social information includes posts, comments, reactions, follows, community membership, optional images and shared-card snapshots. Profile and feed visibility follows the applicable audience, blocking and moderation controls. Other members may retain copies of content you share. Historical card-post snapshots can remain a record of what was shared even after the card changes owner. Reports, internal moderation notes, private preferences and authentication records are not ordinary public posts.

When you contact support we receive your message and information you choose to include. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, verification links or identity documents by email. Real identity-document collection is not enabled in the current test release.

## Purchases and service providers

For an enabled Google Play purchase, Google processes checkout and your payment method. Docked verifies the purchase on its server and records purchase references, product, account binding, status and fulfilment/refund evidence. Docked does not receive your complete Google payment-card number. Purchase references are sensitive and are not public card or profile data. Google processes information under its own privacy terms.

The current private beta has real-money purchases disabled. The paragraph above describes the proposed paid release and must not be used to imply that paid purchasing is currently available. PayPal web checkout has separate implementation and approval controls; if web payments are enabled, the published notice must accurately describe the provider, transaction data and purposes. The Android app must not divert digital-goods purchases to an unapproved external checkout.

Docked uses Supabase for authentication/database services and Vercel for hosting. The existing operational record identifies Microsoft Exchange Online for support/account email, purchased through GoDaddy, including its listed email security service. Confirm these arrangements and the production email transport before publication. Sydney configuration does not mean all processing, backups or support access stays in Australia. Providers may process data overseas through their infrastructure and subprocessors. Publish the verified locations and transfer explanation; do not promise Australian-only processing.

Information may be disclosed to authorised support personnel, advisers, service providers or authorities where reasonably necessary to provide the service, handle a request, investigate an incident or comply with applicable obligations. Document the actual legal basis and access restrictions before publication.

## Device information, analytics and notifications

Network and security operations can involve IP addresses, request/device information and service logs. Optional first-party usage events are sent only after the server confirms analytics consent; the observer respects the browser's Do Not Track setting. Anonymous visitors are not assigned an analytics identity by that observer. This is not a claim that hosting providers collect no operational information.

Android and Google Play may provide diagnostics under their own settings. No new advertising or third-party crash-reporting SDK is part of this release work. Optional media selection and notification preferences follow the application's permissions and server controls. Do not describe push delivery as enabled without verifying its actual configuration. There is no need to grant contacts, microphone or location access to buy a pack.

## Access, correction and deletion

You can request an account export and use Account settings to request deletion. A web request path is available at https://docked.com.au/account-deletion without installing the app. If you cannot sign in, use recovery or contact support. We verify requests proportionately and do not routinely ask for identity documents.

Deletion first revokes account access and active sessions. Identity erasure and eligible content removal follow through the deletion workflow; a failed or deferred step is reported as pending, not complete. The current private beta shares authentication identities with another environment and therefore requires operator review before identity erasure. This limitation must be resolved and tested for the public release.

Some necessary purchase, ownership, competition, consent, moderation and security evidence may need to remain for an applicable legal obligation, dispute or integrity purpose. Limit retained information and access; remove identity links when no longer needed. Still-linkable information is not anonymous. Financial and ownership history must not be silently destroyed to make a deletion counter appear complete.

Publish an approved retention schedule specifying each record category, retention trigger, period or decision rule, reason, responsible operator, backup handling and deletion job. The earlier beta policy's periods are not automatically a suitable public commercial schedule. No statutory period is invented in this draft. Provider backups, holds and recovery systems may have different deletion constraints; explain verified limitations and reapply deletion requests before restored data returns to use.

Contact support for access, correction or a complaint. Explain any lawful restriction and the next review step. Do not publish a response-time promise until the operator confirms staffing and process. Where the Privacy Act applies, information about complaints is available from the OAIC at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints. This notice does not restrict non-excludable rights.

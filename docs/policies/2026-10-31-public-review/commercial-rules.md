# Public commercial rules — owner decision draft

NOT APPROVED, NOT PUBLISHED. Current service remains an invitation-only, non-redeemable test economy.

## Proposed enabled sports and purchases

NFL, AFL and NRL only. NBA is unavailable and absent from listing assets and customer navigation; its stored records remain intact.

The confirmed credit-package contents are A$49 for 4,900 Docked Credits and A$99 for 9,900, with no bonus. Google displays the current purchase price. The single shared account wallet buys the existing six sport-specific Standard/Premium products. No direct pack AUD prices are approved. Existing pack quantities, pools, probabilities, serials and lifetime supply cannot be changed by activation.

Core gameplay eligibility is free and does not itself create a tradable collectible. Optional paid packs exclude Core. Before purchase, show exact card count, eligible rarity pool, configured chances and total. Random selection is not a guarantee of a particular player, rarity, score or financial return.

The current credit-sale fee remains 5%, capped at 2,000 credits, no minimum; nearest whole-credit rounding with half a credit upward. Buyers pay the listed price. Offers and swaps remain disabled. Marketplace resale with purchased funds requires approval of transfer, refund and fraud rules; it is not silently enabled by adding a Play product.

Rarity multipliers are Core 1.0×, Rare 1.1×, Elite 1.2×, Ultra Rare 1.3×, Legendary 1.4×, Icon 1.5×, subject to each competition's saved contract. Salary caps and results remain under their saved versions. Buying a card does not guarantee a win. Postgame/as-available updates must not be marketed as guaranteed live play-by-play.

## Decisions required before commercial activation

1. Confirm commercial player/card identity and statistics rights for the exact public catalogue. Free source visibility is not licensing approval.
2. Obtain legal/platform assessment of the complete paid-card, scoring-multiplier, trading and reward model. Do not infer approval from the availability of Play Billing.
3. Approve provenance separation: existing beta credits/inventory remain test-only; no automatic monetary conversion or new permanent issuance.
4. Approve refunds after credits are spent, unopened refunded-pack treatment, customer notice/review and fraud controls. The tested implementation protects unrelated funds and ownership; it does not invent a debt-collection policy.
5. Approve the public age/jurisdiction, consent, privacy, deletion and moderation policies. Proposed Australian 18+ availability is not an IARC rating.
6. Keep cash features, withdrawals, paid entry and prize awards disabled. Any later activation is a separate decision. Identity verification does not turn these on.
7. Approve the exact tested release, products, store declarations and public registration switch only after hosted customer acceptance.

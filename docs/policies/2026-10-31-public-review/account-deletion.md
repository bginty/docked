# Account deletion — owner review draft

NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED. Companion to the privacy notice, not a claim of completed erasure.

## Proposed customer notice

You can request deletion from Docked's account settings or at https://docked.com.au/account-deletion. You do not need to install the app to use the web request path. If you cannot access your account, use account recovery or contact support@docked.com.au. Do not email your password, identity documents or complete payment details.

We verify that the request comes from the account holder. Account access and sessions are revoked when the deletion request is accepted. Removal of eligible account information and content is processed separately; a pending request is not represented as completed deletion.

Some transaction, ownership, competition, moderation and security records may need to remain for a documented integrity, dispute or legal purpose. Retention must be limited to that purpose and approved schedule. Retained identifiers are protected and are not described as anonymous while they can still be linked to you. Shared content already copied by others cannot necessarily be recalled.

Deletion does not silently cancel a Google order, refund a purchase or erase another member's legitimate ownership. Contact support about an unresolved purchase or remedy. Applicable consumer rights are preserved.

## Publication and operational requirements

- Approve exact categories, retention triggers, periods/decision rules, holds, processor requests and backup/restoration handling. No statutory period is invented here.
- Current private-beta Auth identities are shared across environments. Automated identity erasure is deliberately blocked pending operator review; do not claim it already works for public accounts.
- Validate a separate, synthetic public-mode account: request → session revocation → queue → eligible social/content erasure → Auth erasure → retained minimal audit → final status. Verify retries and processor failure recovery. Do not delete a real tester to prove this.
- The app and web page must show the same accurate completion state. The public page must remain usable without a prior login, with proportionate verification before any destructive action.
- Owner must approve a practical response process and support staffing; no unapproved service timeframe is promised.

Google requires both an in-app deletion path and a web request resource where account creation is offered, including relevant associated data. Deactivation alone must not be presented as erasure. [Google account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111).

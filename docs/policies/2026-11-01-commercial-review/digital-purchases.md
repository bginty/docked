# Docked Credits and card packs

**Proposed public text — owner/legal review; not in force.**

Google Play confirms the current local price before you pay. The proposed Australian credit products are A$49 for 4,900 Docked Credits and A$99 for 9,900 Docked Credits, with no bonus credits. A verified purchase belongs to the authenticated Docked account used for checkout; switching accounts does not transfer the receipt. Credits are usable within Docked and cannot be withdrawn as money.

Standard packs contain 1, 3 or 9 cards for 100, 300 or 700 credits respectively. Premium packs contain 1, 3 or 9 cards for 700, 1,900 or 5,000 credits respectively. The selected sport is shown before confirmation. No credit debit occurs merely by opening purchase review or selecting Buy another pack.

Standard contains Rare, Elite, Ultra Rare, Legendary or Icon cards. Premium contains Ultra Rare, Legendary or Icon cards. Core is excluded from paid packs and remains available for free gameplay. The application displays the authoritative per-card probabilities next to purchase review. The 1/3/9 variants use the same family probability mechanism.

Player selection excludes duplicate players within a pack. Lifetime supply and serial rules apply. If the selected rarities cannot be fulfilled from eligible supply, the complete purchase fails without credit debit or substitution. Disclosed per-card draws are before supply checks; completed-pack frequencies can differ near exhaustion. Opening animations do not determine your cards.

An interrupted response must be reconciled using the same operation; it is not a reason to issue a new pack. Successfully purchased unopened packs remain in My Packs. Opening is idempotent, and re-viewing a receipt does not issue cards again. Approved coupons apply only to their explicitly eligible products; no beta coupon is automatically a commercial coupon.

See the refund conditions for delayed payments, refunds after spending and support review. No expiry or guaranteed cash/resale value is promised by this proposed release.

# Purchase help and refunds

**Proposed public text — owner/legal review; not in force.**

For a Google Play purchase, request help through Google Play or support@docked.com.au. Include the order reference if available, but never your password, verification link or full payment-card details. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law are not excluded by these conditions or by spending credits or opening a pack.

Pending payments do not add spendable credits. If a receipt or connection is delayed, use Check purchases; do not purchase again simply to retry verification. Duplicate delivery of one verified purchase does not add another credit allocation.

When Google confirms a full refund, unused credits from that purchase are removed. Where the funding has already been used, Docked may temporarily restrict further purchases, spending, unopened packs and trading while support reviews the transaction. This is a review restriction, not an accusation of misconduct or an automatic debt. Spendable balances do not become negative. Unrelated members' wallets and cards are not automatically confiscated.

Free gameplay and access to support are not automatically blocked by the payment review. Previously opened pack receipts remain available. Support can release an eligible completed-refund hold following an authorised, recorded review; releasing a hold does not itself issue credits or send money.

Partial refunds and inconsistent provider records require individual reconciliation. Contact support for the outcome, remedy or review of a restriction. A provider refund and a Docked support decision have separate audit records. This policy does not promise an unapproved review time or replace Google's applicable refund processes.

You can request a Google Play refund through https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. Google handles the payment-method refund; Docked reconciles the corresponding in-app entitlement. A missing in-app update is not a reason to pay again. Contact support with the order reference so the existing purchase can be checked. Support will not ask you to repay through an external checkout to remove a hold.

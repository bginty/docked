# Marketplace conditions

**Proposed public text — owner/legal review; commercial activation remains off.**

The private beta's test-credit marketplace is separate from commercial purchases. Commercial sales will not become available unless separately approved and enabled. No cash withdrawal entitlement arises from holding or selling a card.

Where credit sales are enabled, only the currently authorised owner may list an eligible, unlocked card. A 5% selling fee is deducted when your card sells, capped at 2,000 credits per sale. There's no minimum fee. Buyers pay the listed price.

Sell a card for 1,000 credits: the fee is 50 credits and you receive 950 credits. Fees are rounded to the nearest whole credit. Half a credit rounds up. The confirmation displays asking price, fee and proceeds. Historical transactions retain their original recorded fees.

Transfers require atomic payment and ownership settlement. Listings may become unavailable when ownership, eligibility or account restrictions change. No offers, swaps or direct credit transfers are introduced. A refund review does not automatically debit an unrelated member or erase historical ownership records. Contact support for disputes; statutory consumer rights remain applicable.

# Docked privacy notice

**Proposed public text — owner/privacy review; not in force.**

Docked is operated by Ginty United Investments Pty Ltd, ABN 78 606 187 106. Contact support@docked.com.au about privacy, access, correction, deletion or a complaint.

We handle account identifiers, email, profile information, eligibility attestations, country/state, consent records, sessions and security information to operate and protect your account. Optional community posts, images, comments, reactions and follows are handled according to their audience, privacy and moderation settings. Do not send passwords, verification links, payment-card details or identity documents to support.

We record packs, cards, serials, ownership, credits, purchases, refunds, fantasy entries and results to deliver the service, prevent duplicate fulfilment and resolve disputes. A historical shared-card snapshot can remain a record of the original post after the card changes owner; it is not proof of current ownership.

Google processes Android checkout and payment-method details. Docked verifies purchase references, product/order identifiers, account binding, region, amounts, purchase status and refund information on its server. We do not receive your full payment-card number from this integration. Purchase tokens are encrypted server-side; they are not stored in browser storage or sent as analytics events. Google notifications trigger verification, rather than directly changing balances.

Docked uses Supabase for authentication/database services and Vercel for hosting, alongside its configured account/support email providers. Service providers may handle information overseas, including for operations, support and backups. Australian application hosting does not mean every provider processes information only in Australia. Disclosure to authorised staff, providers, advisers or authorities is limited to applicable service, security, dispute or legal purposes.

Optional first-party analytics follows consent and the application's privacy controls. Operational security logs are separate from optional analytics. Media selection is user initiated; buying a pack does not require access to contacts, microphone or precise location. Identity-document collection is not part of this proposed no-withdrawal launch.

Use Account settings to request an export or deletion. A web request is available at https://docked.com.au/account-deletion. If you cannot sign in, use account recovery or contact support. Authentication and proportionate identity checks protect these requests. A request is not reported as completed while required erasure work is still pending.

Some purchase, financial, ownership, dispute, consent or security evidence may be retained where a documented obligation or necessary integrity purpose applies. Retention must be limited to the approved purpose and period. Still-linkable account references are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Backups and documented legal holds may require different handling; restored data must be subject to the same approved deletion controls before reuse.

Contact support to request access/correction or raise a complaint. We will explain applicable restrictions and review options. This policy does not exclude non-excludable privacy or consumer rights. Information about privacy complaints is available from the OAIC at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints.

# Free fantasy competitions

**Proposed public text — owner/rules review; does not activate competitions.**

NFL, AFL and NRL use one Docked account with sport-specific teams, schedules and saved rules. Eligible free Core players are available in PLAY without buying packs or issuing permanent collectible cards. A player's free gameplay eligibility is not collectible ownership.

Traditional enforces the applicable round's salary rules. Approved future configurations are NFL seven players and $4.4m, AFL 22 players and $13.8m, and NRL 13 players and $8.2m. All-Star is uncapped. Salary dollars are not wallet credits. Historical QA rounds keep their saved rules and units.

AFL uses six Backs, eight Midfielders, six Forwards and two Rucks. NRL uses two Fullbacks/Wings, two Centres, two Halves, three Edge Forwards, three Middle Forwards and one Flex accepting any eligible NRL position. Ownership/eligibility, unique players, slot positions and round-wide lockout are validated on the server.

Saved competition rules determine scoring and applicable rarity multipliers. Postgame statistics may arrive progressively and be corrected through the authorised process; missing statistics are not automatically zero. Provisional results are identified. Neither buying credits nor a refund rewrites historical entries or scores.

The proposed public launch has free entry and no prizes. A future prize schedule preview does not create an entitlement or apply retrospectively to a no-prize test round. Public competition eligibility, seasons and editable rounds must be separately configured and approved. NBA is not part of the visible release.

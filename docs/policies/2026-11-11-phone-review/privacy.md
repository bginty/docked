# Docked privacy notice

**Proposed text for founder approval — not published or in force.**

Version: 2026-11-11-privacy-phone-v4. Effective on approved publication.

## Who we are

Docked is operated by Ginty United Investments Pty Ltd, ABN 78 606 187 106. Contact support@docked.com.au about privacy, access, correction, deletion or a complaint.

## Account and gameplay information

We handle account identifiers, email, profile information, eligibility attestations, country/state, consent records, sessions and security information to operate and protect your account. Optional community posts, images, comments, reactions and follows are handled according to their audience, privacy and moderation settings. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or identity documents to support.

We record packs, cards, serials, ownership, credits, purchases, refunds, fantasy entries and results to deliver the service, prevent duplicate fulfilment and resolve disputes. A historical shared-card snapshot can remain a record of the original post after the card changes owner; it is not proof of current ownership.

## Phone verification and processing

For public Australian account use, we require verification of an Australian mobile number. We use the number to establish control of that number, link one verified number to one Docked account, and reduce duplicate accounts and abuse. This does not establish that a person owns only one number or prove Australian residency. Your number is not displayed on your profile. Docked does not use verification numbers for marketing or sell them.

Supabase Auth stores the account's phone number, verification status and related authentication information. Twilio receives the destination number and verification message to deliver it through telecommunications providers. Verification messages contain a short-lived code. Enter the code only in Docked; do not give it to support or another person. Docked does not request access to your contacts or SMS inbox for verification. Twilio also processes delivery records and communications metadata for its own service operations, security, abuse prevention, service improvement and legal obligations under its published terms.

## Existing users and account recovery

Existing accounts retain their identities and card ownership. Where public account features require phone verification, existing users must complete it too. A phone number does not replace your password, verified email or an enrolled authenticator. If your number is lost, changed or reassigned, contact support@docked.com.au. We use account-authentication and ownership checks before assisting recovery; possession of a reassigned number alone does not authorise transfer of another account, cards or credits. Account-deletion requests remain available without completing phone verification.

## Social content and support

Posts, comments, reactions, follows, community memberships, optional images and shared-card snapshots are processed to provide the social features. Visibility follows audience, privacy, blocking and moderation controls. Other members may copy content you share. A historical card-post snapshot can remain a record of what was shared after a transfer. Reports, internal moderation notes, private preferences and authentication records are not ordinary public posts.

Support receives the messages and information you provide. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, verification links or identity documents. Real identity-document collection is disabled in the current test release.

## Purchases

Until commercial launch approval, real-money purchasing remains disabled and only authorised Google Play TEST checkout is available. When approved purchasing is enabled, Google handles checkout and payment methods. Docked's server verifies purchase references, product, bound account, purchase state, fulfilment and refund evidence. Docked does not receive a complete Google payment-card number. Purchase references are sensitive and are not public profile data. Google processes information under its own privacy terms. Paid purchasing remains subject to the payment and commercial approval process. This notice does not activate purchases.

Google processes Android checkout and payment-method details. Docked verifies purchase references, product/order identifiers, account binding, region, amounts, purchase status and refunds on its server. We do not receive your full payment-card number from this integration. Purchase tokens are encrypted server-side and are not stored in browser storage or sent as analytics events. Google notifications trigger verification rather than directly changing balances.

## Service providers and overseas processing

Docked uses Supabase for authentication/database services, Vercel for hosting, Twilio for verification SMS and configured account/support email providers. Supabase's selected database region is Australia; providers may process information outside Australia, including in the United States and Singapore for delivery, operations or support. The current Twilio messaging service uses its United States region. Australian database hosting does not mean every provider processes information only in Australia. Provider and telecommunications processing is subject to their applicable privacy terms and legal obligations. Disclosure to authorised staff, advisers or authorities is limited to service, security, dispute or legal purposes.

Our support/account mailbox uses Microsoft Exchange Online, purchased through GoDaddy with its listed email security service. We have not verified the mailbox's data-at-rest geography or every provider recovery, quarantine, backup or hold setting. We do not promise Australian-only processing or a fixed date when every provider-held copy is erased.

## Device information and analytics

Network and security operations can involve IP addresses, device/request information and service logs. Optional first-party usage events require analytics consent and respect the browser's Do Not Track setting. Anonymous visitors are not assigned an identity by that observer. This does not mean hosting providers collect no operational information.

The app has no advertising SDK. Android and Google Play may provide operating-system diagnostics under their own settings.

The Android app also includes Google Play Billing Library. When the library is initialised or used, it sends billing-operation results, technical app/device information and a per-session identifier to Google for billing reliability, performance improvement and error support. This automatic SDK processing can occur without a completed purchase. It is separate from Docked's optional usage analytics and is not disabled by that analytics preference. Google handles this information under its own privacy terms.

Optional media selection and notification preferences follow device permissions and application controls. Contacts, microphone and location access are not required to buy a pack. We do not use identity documents or purchase tokens in Docked's optional usage analytics.

## Security

The app uses HTTPS for communications with its servers. Access restrictions, rate limits, verification checks and privileged-account MFA help protect information. Ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. Optional first-party analytics follows consent and the application's privacy controls; security logs are separate. Media selection is user initiated. Buying a pack does not require contacts, microphone or precise-location access. Identity-document collection is not part of this no-withdrawal launch.

## Phone and verification-message retention

We retain the current account phone number while it is needed for account verification and security. Account deletion removes active account identifying information through the account-erasure process; necessary restricted evidence may remain for financial, security, dispute or legal purposes. Verification-message records are separate from the active account record. Twilio's published default makes message records available for 400 days and can retain older records in archives until deletion is requested or configured. After a message is deleted, Twilio says its body may remain in backups for up to 30 days. Telecommunications records and data held for independent legal or security purposes can have different retention periods. A code expiring does not delete the message or its delivery record. No automatic 90-day deletion of phone or SMS records is promised.

## Access, correction and account deletion

Request an account export or deletion through Account settings. The account-deletion page is accessible on the web without installing the app. If you cannot sign in, use account recovery or contact support. We verify requests proportionately and do not routinely ask for identity documents.

Deletion first revokes access and sessions. In the current private beta, shared authentication identities require operator review before identity erasure; access revocation alone is not completed deletion. Eligible personal content is removed or pseudonymised through the deletion workflow. A failed or deferred step remains pending. Necessary ownership, competition, consent, dispute and security evidence can be retained for its documented purpose. Still-linkable information is not anonymous. Our retention notice explains the current targets and limitations.

For public customer accounts, credential erasure removes sign-in secrets and identifying Auth metadata while an opaque account reference may remain linked to necessary ownership, competition and audit records. The active profile is removed. This reference is restricted, is not an active login and is not described as anonymous while still linkable.

Use Account settings to request an export or deletion, or https://docked.com.au/account-deletion for a web request. If you cannot sign in, use account recovery or contact support. Authentication and proportionate checks protect these requests. Tell support if your request includes verification-message records so relevant provider-held records can be assessed and requests coordinated. We explain records that must be retained and why. A pending request is not represented as completed.

## Financial records and ownership provenance

Company financial records are retained for at least seven years where required by Australian company law. This does not apply automatically to every profile detail, image, phone number or purchase token. Necessary minimal card serial and ownership provenance can remain after account deletion to protect other owners and permanent supply. Still-linkable account references are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Documented legal holds can extend the relevant period. Backups can retain information until expiry; recovery controls must honour completed deletion requests before restored information is returned to use.

## Questions and complaints

Contact support to request access or correction or raise a complaint. We will explain applicable restrictions and review options. This notice does not exclude non-excludable privacy or consumer rights. Privacy complaint information is available from the OAIC at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints. Previous accepted policy versions remain recorded; publication does not rewrite a past consent.

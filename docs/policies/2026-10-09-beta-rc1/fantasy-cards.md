Cards are server-recorded game items, not NFTs or investments. The initial player catalogue is fictional and must be labelled. Each edition has a predefined finite supply; serials and ownership history must be immutable. Rarity must not multiply performance points. Buying, selling and trading remain disabled.

Beta editions, allocation pools, packs, claims, ownership and receipts must be isolated from official records before release. Current shared supply and lifetime starter entitlements do not satisfy that requirement. The stock quantities below are inherited candidate limits, not populated beta inventory or a guarantee that stock exists.

- **Starter:** one free pack of 11 CORE cards per eligible verified member account, claimed after the required onboarding/consent. The formation allocation is 1 goalkeeper, 4 defenders, 4 midfielders and 2 forwards, with unique players within the pack. Claims are permanently recorded and cannot be reset by retries or later inventory releases. Initial allocation is 1,000 Starter packs; additional bounded releases require an authorized MFA administrator and cannot increase permanent edition caps. If stock is unavailable, no successful claim is recorded. The launch homepage must disclose availability rather than promise unlimited immediate stock.
- **Daily:** claim 10 non-transferable gameplay points once in each UTC calendar day after claiming the Starter. The server displays the next claim time. Missed periods are not backdated. A repeated request returns the original result; it does not grant another reward. A local calendar day can differ from the UTC reward period.
- **Every seventh successful claim:** a controlled one-card CORE reward pack is available subject to remaining inventory and the shared daily card limit. These are seven successful claims, not necessarily seven consecutive days. Initial limits are 100 reward cards across all members per UTC day and 10,000 reward packs in the initial allocation. When card stock or the daily card limit is exhausted, the daily points still apply and the receipt records the reason no card pack was awarded. That receipt does not create an automatic later card entitlement.
- **Permanent records:** each claim records its applicable reward-policy version. The member view shows recent history; the database retains permanent claim and card-provenance records. Neither points nor card rarity changes fantasy performance scoring. No cash value is promised.
- **Changes:** reward configuration can be changed only through authorized MFA administration within the implemented bounds. The launch configuration remains 10 points and a seven-claim interval. Any future member-facing change must be communicated accurately and must not rewrite previous receipts or increase a launched edition's supply cap.

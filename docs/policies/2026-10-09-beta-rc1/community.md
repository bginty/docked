## Complaints and privacy requests — proposed operating procedure

Email **support@docked.com.au** with “Docked complaint”, “Moderation appeal” or “Privacy request”, a short description, relevant dates and the outcome sought. Provide your username or account email where needed, not passwords, verification links, payment information or unnecessary identity documents. A representative may be asked for appropriate authority. Docked may request proportionate information to verify an account-related request without routinely collecting government identity documents.

The proposed procedure is to acknowledge receipt, assign an authorized person, assess the relevant records and explain the outcome and available review options. We aim to respond within 30 calendar days; if more time is needed, explain why and provide an update. For a moderation appeal, use a different reviewer where practicable. Record only the information needed to handle the complaint and any justified follow-up. These commitments require an assigned owner and approval before publication.

Where the Privacy Act applies, a person who is dissatisfied with Docked's response, or has received no response after 30 days, may complain to the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints/lodge-a-privacy-complaint-with-us). This does not claim Docked belongs to an external dispute resolution scheme. Consumers may also seek advice from their state or territory consumer-protection authority. Applicable rights are not limited by this procedure.

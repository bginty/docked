# Community Guidelines

Version 2026-10-11-beta-rc3. Effective with free NFL friends-beta activation.

Be respectful. Do not post harassment, threats, hate, exploitation, pornography, graphic violence, encouragement of self-harm, scams, illegal material, impersonation or private information without permission. Share only content you have permission to use. Do not promote paid sales, gambling, external reward-farming services or cash redemption of beta cards, points or accounts. Do not manipulate scores or encourage security exploits.

Use report, block and mute controls. Reports and private preferences are not public posts. Uploaded images are subject to moderation. The owner may restrict content or accounts with an audited reason. Request review through support@docked.com.au without reposting harmful material. Include your username, relevant dates and the outcome sought, not passwords, verification links or identity documents.

The owner checks support daily, aims to acknowledge routine requests within two business days and resolve routine privacy/complaint requests within 30 days, explaining delays. Another reviewer is used where practicable. Urgent security/safety reports receive prompt containment and review; continuous moderation is not promised. For immediate danger, contact emergency services. Suspected data breaches or applicable reporting obligations are escalated for specialist assessment. Applicable rights are not limited by this procedure.

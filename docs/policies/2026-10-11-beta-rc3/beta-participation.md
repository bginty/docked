# Free Beta Participation

Version 2026-10-11-beta-rc3. Effective with free NFL friends-beta activation.

The beta admits only the owner and at most ten invited Australian adults aged 18+. Invitations are email-bound, expiring and single-use. The owner authorises recipients individually; public self-registration is closed. Ordinary members are not required to enrol in MFA; owner/admin access requires MFA.

NFL cards, teams, free card swaps and post-game scoring are the initial scope. EPL is unavailable. Beta cards and points have no monetary value. Payments, paid packs, cash trading, prizes, withdrawals and identity-document collection are disabled. Do not sell or share accounts or invitations.

Historical acceptance competitions and simulations are isolated and labelled; they are not genuine member results. Beta records do not automatically transfer to a future commercial product. Report defects, content or access concerns to support@docked.com.au. Device testing and service reliability may still reveal beta limitations; no uninterrupted service is promised.

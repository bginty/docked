Version 2026-10-11-beta-rc3. Applies when the free NFL friends beta is activated; it does not change earlier consent records.

## Information and purposes

The free beta is invitation-only for the owner and at most ten Australian adults. We collect your account email and identifiers, profile, country/state and age attestations, consent versions, preferences, authentication/security records, cards and ownership history, teams, competition entries, scores and community activity. You provide account and community information; the service generates gameplay and security records. We use these to run the game, protect accounts and finite inventory, moderate content, handle requests and record consent. Required account and eligibility information is needed to participate; optional profile content is your choice.

Profiles, posts and competition information follow the app's visibility and blocking controls. Reports, private preferences and security records are not public posts. Do not submit passwords, verification links, identity documents, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information to support. Payments, prizes, cash trading, withdrawals and identity-document collection are disabled. Ordinary members do not need mandatory MFA; administrators do.

## Providers and locations

We use Supabase for authentication and the database configured in Sydney, Vercel for web hosting with Sydney functions, and Microsoft Exchange Online for support and account email. The mailbox was purchased through GoDaddy, which also lists Advanced Email Security. Providers may use overseas infrastructure, support and subprocessors. Supabase's published residency guidance identifies additional support/service processing in the United States, Singapore and Europe; these are not the only possible locations across all providers. Sydney configuration does not mean all processing stays in Australia.

We have not verified this mailbox's data-at-rest geography or every provider recovery, security-quarantine, backup or hold setting. We do not promise Australian-only processing or a fixed date when every provider-held copy is erased. We will update this notice when material location or preservation information is confirmed. Necessary information may also be disclosed to authorised service/support personnel, advisers or authorities where required to handle a request, incident or applicable obligation.

## Access, closure and retention

Contact support for an account-related export, correction or closure. We verify requests proportionately without routinely collecting identity documents. A support-assisted export covers available account/gameplay records and selected support correspondence; the self-service export may not contain every historical record. We explain any restriction needed to protect another person or satisfy an applicable obligation.

On verified closure we revoke beta access immediately. We aim to remove eligible account/contact details and posts/media within 30 days. Ongoing competitions, disputes and documented incidents can require limited records for longer; we explain the reason and review date. We remove account-to-game identity links within 30 days after relevant rounds/disputes close. Remaining minimised ownership/scoring evidence is reviewed at beta end and deleted or further de-identified within 12 months of beta closure unless a documented exception remains. Still-linkable records are not described as anonymous. Permanent supply and scoring integrity do not justify keeping names or email addresses indefinitely.

Routine support cases are scheduled for removal from the active mailbox 12 months after closure. Exported working copies are removed after delivery verification, within seven days. Routine operational-log retention has a maximum target of 30 days and security logs 90 days; some provider logs expire sooner. Exceptional incident/dispute evidence requires a recorded reason and review at least every 90 days. These periods describe Docked's handling of records it controls, not verified automatic expiry for every provider copy.

Recovery, quarantine, backup and applicable holds may preserve copies after active deletion. We investigate relevant restrictions for each request, explain known limitations, restrict unnecessary continued use and reapply completed deletion requests before restored data returns to active use. We do not promise immediate or guaranteed complete provider erasure within the active-record targets above.

## Support and complaints

The owner operates support/moderation, checks the inbox daily, aims to acknowledge routine requests within two business days and resolve routine privacy requests within 30 days. If more time is needed, we explain why and provide an update. Ask for review of a disputed response; we use another reviewer where practicable. Urgent safety/security reports receive prompt containment and review. Contact emergency services for immediate danger. Suspected breaches are escalated for specialist assessment where needed.

Where the Privacy Act applies, a dissatisfied person may seek assistance from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints. This notice does not restrict rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Optional marketing remains disabled and no historical storefront mailing list is imported into the beta.

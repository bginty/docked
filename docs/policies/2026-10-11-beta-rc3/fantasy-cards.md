# Fantasy Card Rules

Version 2026-10-11-beta-rc3. Effective with free NFL friends-beta activation.

All current NFL players are eligible under the beta catalogue rules, with supported playable positions and verified mappings required for allocation. The server allocates a valid starter collection of ten distinct players: two quarterbacks, three running backs, three wide receivers and two tight ends. Allocation is free, audited and idempotent; retrying does not issue another entitlement. Availability is checked before issuing cards. No first-year-only rule applies to this isolated free beta.

Beta inventory is separate from permanent commercial editions. Existing fictional cards/history are retained separately, never silently renamed. Each card retains its identity, edition, serial and ownership provenance. Transfers cannot replenish lifetime issuance capacity. Rarity has no effect on match points.

Permitted card-for-card beta swaps are free. The server checks both owners and eligibility, transfers atomically and rejects stale, cancelled, expired or competing offers. Cash sales and paid fees are disabled. Transfers do not rewrite locked lineup snapshots or earned points, and the implemented locked-card restrictions apply. A card does not guarantee eligibility in every future competition.

Cards, engagement points and beta rewards have no monetary value or cash redemption. Any supported engagement reward is separate from sporting scores. The current NFL allocation/scoring rules, not historical fictional reward configurations, govern this beta. Contact support@docked.com.au for missing allocations or ownership concerns.

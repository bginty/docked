# Competition and Points Rules

Version 2026-10-11-beta-rc3. Effective with free NFL friends-beta activation.

NFL teams use seven distinct eligible owned players: one QB, two RB, two WR, one TE and one FLEX from RB/WR/TE. The server enforces positions, ownership and the displayed whole-round deadline. No full round opens after its first fixture has started. The displayed deadline uses local time; the server clock is authoritative.

Each competition records its scoring/rules version and locks a lineup snapshot. Later transfers or lineup edits cannot alter that snapshot or previously earned points. Match points are equal across card tiers. No captain, rarity, level, price or spend multiplier applies. Engagement points are separate and never added to sporting scores.

The scoring tables at /fantasy/scoring are generated from Docked's versioned engine. NFL uses post-game source statistics, not live fantasy totals. Collection begins no earlier than six hours after Docked first observes a reliable completed-game marker; that observation time is not an exact final-whistle timestamp. Low-frequency schedules and source availability can delay scores further.

Missing or ambiguous required fields remain awaiting results/pending or disputed, never invented final zeroes. Provisional totals may change after source correction. Final standings require required results to be complete and finalised; unresolved ties are shown without inventing a winner. Append-only revisions preserve correction history. There are no entry fees or prizes. Historical acceptance fixtures remain separate from genuine member competitions.

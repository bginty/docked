## Restricted beta scope

Australia only; invitation-only for the owner and at most ten invited testers, all aged 18+. Participation is free. Public registration, deposits, withdrawals, paid cards/packs, marketplace transactions, cash prizes and rewards with monetary value are disabled. Beta cards and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or sold. Beta inventory, entitlements, transactions, statistics and rankings remain separate from official records. No unvalidated official betting recommendations are published. The owner handles support and moderation; the designated administrator and contact is support@docked.com.au.

The operator above is responsible for Docked's handling of personal information. Docked uses account email and identifiers, username/profile, self-attested age and country/state, consent versions, preferences, authentication and security records. Passwords are submitted to the authentication service. The game stores card allocations and ownership history, pack claims, gameplay points, teams, competitions and results. Social features store submitted posts/comments/images, relationships, reactions, saves, reports, blocks, mutes and notifications. Do not submit identity documents, payment-card details, sportsbook credentials or precise GPS location; these are not needed for the approved launch.

Information is used to authenticate accounts, run the game, enforce scarcity and ownership, prevent abuse, moderate social features, respond to support requests and record consent. Profile/social visibility follows the product's access and blocking rules; private preferences, reports and saves are not public. Optional analytics and marketing consent remain separate from necessary account/security processing. Optional outbound marketing and Android push are not enabled by this release. Historical storefront mailing lists are not imported into Fantasy communications.

The intended production providers are now Vercel for website delivery, Supabase for authentication/database services in Sydney, and Microsoft 365 for support and transactional email. Netlify is retained but is no longer the selected beta host. Only necessary data should be sent to each provider. Supabase's current residency guidance describes regional database/Auth/storage and backup hosting, with some support, service metadata and delivery processing outside the region, including US/Singapore support and EU logs. Vercel functions are configured for Sydney; this alone does not establish exclusive Australian processing. Review the applicable Vercel subprocessors and processing locations before finalizing the overseas disclosure. Microsoft mailbox geography depends on the tenant's actual configuration, which still needs administrator verification. The launch policy must identify likely overseas recipients/countries where practicable; it must not promise that all data stays in Australia. The restricted beta uses separate gameplay namespaces inside the dedicated production project, with shared authentication identity services. The older isolated Preview database is not used for these accounts.

Members can use the available account controls to change preferences, export data and request deletion. The operator must verify these flows against production before promising them publicly. Deletion must revoke access and remove or pseudonymise eligible profile/social data while preserving the minimum lawful audit and scarcity evidence. Pseudonymous identifiers may still be personal information; an immutable transaction requirement is not a blanket justification to retain all personal information forever. Final retention/destruction periods, backup expiry, exceptions/legal holds and account-to-ledger unlinking need documented review and implementation verification.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy concerns, contact support@docked.com.au using the complaints procedure in the Community Guidelines. Requested access may need to be limited to protect another person's information or satisfy applicable law; explain a refusal and review options where required. Security incidents must be assessed promptly, contained and notified to affected people/regulators when applicable legal thresholds are met. The owner is the proposed contact for privacy requests and incident coordination as part of the operating commitments in this approval packet.

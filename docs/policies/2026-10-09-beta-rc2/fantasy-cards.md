## Restricted beta scope

Australia only; invitation-only for the owner and at most ten invited testers, all aged 18+. Participation is free. Public registration, deposits, withdrawals, paid cards/packs, marketplace transactions, cash prizes and rewards with monetary value are disabled. Beta cards and points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed or sold. Beta inventory, entitlements, transactions, statistics and rankings remain separate from official records. No unvalidated official betting recommendations are published. The owner handles support and moderation; the designated administrator and contact is support@docked.com.au.

Cards are server-recorded game items, not NFTs or investments. The initial player catalogue is fictional and must be labelled. Each edition has a predefined finite supply; serials and ownership history must be immutable. Rarity must not multiply performance points. Buying, selling and trading remain disabled.

Beta editions, allocation pools, packs, lifetime starter entitlements, claims, ownership and receipts now use separate database namespaces. Beta use does not consume official inventory or official account entitlements. The stock quantities below are beta configuration ceilings, not invitation places or a guarantee of currently available stock. Finite beta inventory must be initialized and verified before claims are enabled.

- **Starter:** one free pack of 11 CORE cards per eligible verified beta member account, claimed after the required onboarding/consent. The formation allocation is 1 goalkeeper, 4 defenders, 4 midfielders and 2 forwards, with unique players within the pack. Claims are permanently recorded and cannot be reset by retries or later inventory releases. Initial allocation is 1,000 Starter packs; additional bounded releases require an authorized MFA administrator and cannot increase permanent edition caps. If stock is unavailable, no successful claim is recorded. The launch homepage must disclose availability rather than promise unlimited immediate stock.
- **Daily:** claim 10 non-transferable gameplay points once in each UTC calendar day after claiming the Starter. The server displays the next claim time. Missed periods are not backdated. A repeated request returns the original result; it does not grant another reward. A local calendar day can differ from the UTC reward period.
- **Every seventh successful claim:** a controlled one-card CORE reward pack is available subject to remaining inventory and the beta-wide daily card limit. These are seven successful claims, not necessarily seven consecutive days. Initial limits are 100 reward cards across all members per UTC day and 10,000 reward packs in the initial allocation. When card stock or the daily card limit is exhausted, the daily points still apply and the receipt records the reason no card pack was awarded. That receipt does not create an automatic later card entitlement.
- **Permanent records:** each claim records its applicable reward-policy version. The member view shows recent history; the database retains permanent claim and card-provenance records. Neither points nor card rarity changes fantasy performance scoring. Cards and points have no monetary value, cash redemption, prize conversion or permitted resale.
- **Changes:** reward configuration can be changed only through authorized MFA administration within the implemented bounds. The launch configuration remains 10 points and a seven-claim interval. Any future member-facing change must be communicated accurately and must not rewrite previous receipts or increase a launched edition's supply cap.

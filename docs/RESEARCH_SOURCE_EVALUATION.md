# Research source evaluation — 4 October 2026

**Free-first recommendation:** assess OpenFootball's CC0 EPL goals/results datasets before buying a sporting feed. Its licence supports the proposed retained, derived research use. Its data quality does not yet establish operational fixture, model or settlement readiness. Optional weather can use the Met Office Free plan after owner account/configuration. Injuries, lineups and xG are not prerequisites for the proposed simple V1.

This review inspected first-party terms, documentation and public repository metadata. Two small pinned OpenFootball files were downloaded into ignored local research storage for quality inspection; no provider account, subscription, paid trial, hosted sporting import or model activation occurred. Prices are advertised prices, not invoices. Unknown rights remain unknown. An engineering source review does not grant league trademarks, clear product gambling laws, or approve public official Edges.

## Reproducible inspection and next decision

The inspected OpenFootball revision is `e6744429ee395bc86f247348c6184bb08d4eb361`, committed 22 September 2026. Files were observed on 4 October 2026; that is Docked's retrieval time, **not** when each score became known. The [2025/26 quality receipt](qa/phase5c/openfootball-2025-26-quality.json) records 380 rows/20 teams/380 reported scores, including 27 unlabelled score tuples. The [2026/27 receipt](qa/phase5c/openfootball-2026-27-quality.json) records 380 rows/20 teams/50 reported scores, including five unlabelled tuples; absent scores are not zeroes or confirmed pending statuses. Both lack timezone, original result timestamps and correction/finality evidence. The adapter retains the distinction between `ft` fields and unlabelled tuples. No downloaded historical match becomes a historical Docked prediction, Edge or ROI entry.

Reproduce offline after obtaining the exact licensed file: `npm run research:source-check -- --file <local-json> --observed-at <actual-retrieval-ISO-time> --revision <commit> --out <new-report.json>` (use `npm.cmd` in PowerShell). Reports use exclusive creation so prior evidence is not overwritten. The command has no network, database, model or publication capability.

Keep all runtime source/schedule activation off until audited registration and dataset mapping/quality acceptance. Source review renewals are engineering policy dates, not claimed licence expiry dates. OpenFootball can be evaluated at $0 without credentials; do this before deciding that a paid feed is necessary. If current authoritative fixture/result needs remain unmet, seek the narrow football-data.org rights clarification and account key next. Weather, player data and news are optional and should not delay a defensible goals-only research model.

No spending is required for the delivered foundation. Any later paid plan, paid trial, extra credits or production activation requires a separate owner decision.

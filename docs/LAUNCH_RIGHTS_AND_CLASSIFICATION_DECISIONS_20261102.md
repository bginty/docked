- No enabled withdrawal, redemption-to-cash, paid competition entry or real-world prize flow.

The saved “Trading/selling of digital goods for real-world currency” summary is therefore not evidence that Docked enables cash-out and must not be changed to No merely because withdrawals are disabled. Disclose paid randomised packs, credit marketplace transfers where enabled, chat and user-generated content accurately.

## Classification and policy conditions

1. Australian games with paid purchases linked to chance have a minimum M classification; simulated gambling has a different R18+ rule. [Government announcement](https://minister.infrastructure.gov.au/rowland/media-release/new-mandatory-minimum-classifications-gambling-games-content). The last verified Console already showed M, chat/online interactivity and chance-based purchases. M is not a gambling licence or an 18+ legal restriction; Docked's intended adult audience is a separate product policy.
2. Under [Google Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738), digital credits use Play Billing, currency stays within this title, and random-item odds must be disclosed near purchase. The approved products are A$49/4,900 and A$99/9,900, not direct A$49/A$99 pack prices.
3. [Google real-money games policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032) addresses staking money or purchased items for prizes of real-world value. The first release should retain free Core entry, no paid entry, no cash-out and no prizes. Do not describe paid packs as gambling solely because random, or declare the linked paid-card/multiplier/transfer model permitted without assessing the full mechanics.
4. The exact external legal question is whether consideration, chance/mixed skill and a prize of money or anything of value arise in the enabled design under the [Interactive Gambling Act](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00851/latest) and applicable state/territory rules, including closed-loop resale and off-platform-value risk. No affirmative clearance is evidenced. Seek a scoped opinion on that configuration, not a generic gambling certificate.
5. Refund holds must not remove statutory remedies. [ACCC consumer guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees) cannot be excluded by a blanket “opened packs are non-refundable” rule. Support needs documented evidence review, appeal and release-of-hold procedures; no automatic bank charge or unrelated owner clawback.

## Strongest available launch configuration

Prepare free app, free Core gameplay, free entry, optional approved paid packs through Play Billing, published odds and rarity multipliers. Keep commercial transfers, withdrawals, paid entry and all prize awards off initially; retain existing beta records in their isolated scope. Use licensed factual roster/statistics packages, owner-confirmed names and generic artwork. This preserves the paid-pack model while removing enabled cash-value reward paths. It remains conditional on the separate legal/platform assessment and final hosted acceptance; it is not approval to launch now.

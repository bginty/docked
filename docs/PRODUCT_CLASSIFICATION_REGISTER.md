# Docked product classification register

Status: **DRAFT — NO CLASSIFICATION DECISION APPROVED; NO ACTIVE SKU**  
Register date: 13 August 2026

This is a screening register, not legal advice, a certificate or a final applicable/not-applicable decision. A competent Australian product-safety/legal reviewer must assess the exact final supplier model, SKU, design, size, capacity, normal and foreseeable use, supplier marketing, packaging, instructions, imagery and age grading. Calling a product “adults only” does not determine its classification.

The official [aquatic toys mandatory-standard page](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/business/search-mandatory-standards/aquatic-toys-mandatory-standard) states that scope turns on, among other matters, whether a product can bear the mass of a child under 14 and is for play in shallow water. It also notes a separate regime for [swimming and flotation aids](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/business/search-mandatory-standards/swimming-and-flotation-aids-mandatory-standard). The reviewer must confirm the law and any transition arrangements current on the intended supply date.

Electrical and radio screening must use the final configuration. Relevant official starting points include the [EESS selling steps](https://www.eess.gov.au/equipment/selling-electrical-equipment-in-australia/steps-for-compliance-with-eess/) and [ACMA supplier obligations](https://www.acma.gov.au/know-what-you-must-do). An RCM image, CE/FCC mark or overseas supplier declaration alone is not an Australian applicability or compliance decision.

## Required assessment questions for every final SKU

Record evidence and reasoning for:

1. Physical design, dimensions, seating/attachment, buoyancy source and component layout.
2. Verified occupancy, maximum load and whether the product can bear a child under 14.
3. Normal and reasonably foreseeable use or misuse, including likely shallow-water play.
4. Supplier/manufacturer marketing, packaging, instructions, imagery and age grading.
5. Aquatic-toy, swimming/flotation-aid, PFD and any other regulated-product scope.
6. Applicable construction, valve, performance, testing, marking and warning requirements.
7. Electrical product/charger scope, EESS risk level, responsible supplier, evidence and RCM basis.
8. Battery chemistry/ratings/safety/transport obligations and carrier acceptance.
9. Radio/EMC scope where a transmitter or receiver exists, including frequency, power, records and labelling.
10. Any material, chemical, canopy, cord/net, entanglement, propulsion, repair-product or performance-claim issue.

## Final decision record

The adviser must replace the screening entry with a controlled record containing:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Final supplier/model/SKU/revision | Exact identifiers and variants covered |
| Product evidence reviewed | Design, dimensions, capacity, materials, packaging, labels, manual, marketing and imagery references |
| Normal and foreseeable use analysis | Including child-mass and shallow-water considerations where relevant |
| Aquatic-toy decision | Applicable/not applicable, reasons, current legal instrument/standard and transition basis |
| Swimming/flotation-aid/PFD decision | Applicable/not applicable and reasons |
| Other product-safety regimes | Applicable/not applicable and reasons |
| Electrical/EESS/RCM decision | Product, charger and accessories separately |
| Battery/transport decision | Exact battery and packing configuration |
| Radio/EMC decision | Controller/device function, frequency/power and applicable obligations |
| Evidence gaps/conditions | Every condition required before supply |
| Reviewer | Name, organisation, competence/scope and signature |
| Review date and recheck trigger | Date plus expiry, legal update and material-change triggers |
| Owner release | Separate approval after all conditions close |

No row in this register may show `Approved` or `Active` until that final decision record and all supporting evidence are complete.

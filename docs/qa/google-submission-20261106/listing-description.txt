Docked brings fantasy teams and digital sports-card collecting into one account for Australian adults aged 18 and over.

Play across NFL, AFL and NRL. Build eligible teams using free Core player selections and enter free fantasy competitions without buying packs. Follow your lineup, available player statistics and round rankings. Free Core selections are gameplay eligibility, not automatically owned collectible cards.

Optional purchases: use Google Play Billing to buy Docked Credits, then choose Standard or Premium digital card packs. Pack contents are random. Card quantities, credit prices and rarity probabilities are shown before confirmation. Rarity multipliers apply under the saved competition rules; paid cards can change a player’s fantasy contribution. Purchases are not required to participate.

View your owned cards and pack history, manage your teams, and connect with members through posts, comments, reactions and communities. Your account and eligible collections are shared with docked.com.au. Email confirmation and any required multi-factor authentication still apply.

Docked Credits are for use within Docked. There are no withdrawals, paid competition entries or cash prizes in this release. Cards do not promise a resale value or investment return. Clearly labelled practice fixtures are separate from official results. Statistics update when verified data is available; Docked does not promise live play-by-play coverage.

Refund support remains available after opening a pack. Confirmed refunds of spent credits can place restricted purchase or transfer activity under review; statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. See the purchase terms and refund policy in the app.

DOCKED — stable season catalogue correction, 15 November 2026

Root cause
The NFL weekly query required both nflverse observations and official roster
evidence within 72 hours, and current active status. A card-insert trigger repeated
the official check. Once collection stopped, 498 candidates became zero eligible.
The collection review expired on 10 November. ba5154ec proposed another short
roster collection window; that would only postpone this same failure.

Separate responsibilities
1. Season membership: immutable approved identity snapshot, canonical player ID,
   provider ID, position, historical team, original evidence and approval reference.
2. Scarcity: existing lifetime commercial definitions/counters, provider-identity
   uniqueness, serial locks and legacy consumption. Season membership never resets
   a counter. Rare 5,000; Elite 2,500; Ultra Rare 400; Legendary 50; Icon 1.
   Core is unlimited; the old NFL Core edition ceiling is no longer an issuance
   cap. Existing serials stay intact and subsequent Core serials remain sequential.
3. Participation: current team, injury, reserve, release and transfer observations
   stay in their original live tables. They do not edit approved card snapshots.
4. Statistics/scoring: existing provider, freshness, fixture and permission checks
   remain authoritative. The six disabled Practice competitions are unchanged.
5. Rights: immutable scoped source-use grants plus append-only identity/rights
   restrictions. Actual expiry/revocation blocks new draws; existing ownership and
   receipts remain readable. Collection permission is a separate prerequisite to
   fetching new material. Missing provider responses do not revoke membership.

Purchases and refunds
2026-11-17-purchases-paid-launch-v1
Australian commercial release. Google Play handles Android credit purchases. Consumer rights and the refund safeguards below apply.

Current availability
Australian members aged 18 or over with an eligible, phone-verified account can purchase Docked Credits through Google Play and use them for Standard and Premium card packs. The checkout identifies the price and credit amount before confirmation. Docked Credits are not cash and cannot be withdrawn. Test credits, TEST orders and isolated test inventory remain separate from purchased credits and commercial packs. Paid competition entry, prizes and withdrawals are unavailable.

Credit packages and pack contents
Google Play credit packages are A$49 for 4,900 Docked Credits and A$99 for 9,900, with no bonus credits. These Australian dollar prices include 10% GST; no extra GST is added at checkout. No separate charge is made when freely granted test credits are issued. Google checkout displays the current price before confirmation. Credits can be used for NFL, AFL or NRL packs. Packs are priced in credits.

Standard packs: 1 card for 100 credits; 3 for 300; 9 for 700. Premium packs: 1 card for 700 credits; 3 for 1,900; 9 for 5,000. A 9-card pack delivers nine permanent digital cards in one successful fulfilment. Review the sport, exact card count, price and available balance before confirming. A 4,900-credit package alone does not cover a 5,000-credit Premium 9-card pack.

For each Standard card slot, the rarity probabilities are Rare 85%, Elite 12%, Ultra Rare 2.5%, Legendary 0.49% and Icon 0.01%. For each Premium card slot, they are Ultra Rare 90%, Legendary 9.9% and Icon 0.1%. Core cards are excluded. These are probabilities for each card slot, not a guarantee of any rarity or player in a pack. A pack contains no duplicate player. If the required eligible supply cannot support the complete pack, the purchase fails without a partial debit or partial delivery; probabilities are not silently substituted. Repeating a completed request does not debit or deliver twice.

Supply limits apply permanently per player and rarity across issuances: Core unlimited; Rare 5,000; Elite 2,500; Ultra Rare 400; Legendary 50; Icon 1. Existing legitimate issued copies consume these limits. Transfers, refunds and a player leaving a roster do not reset lifetime issuance. Cards are digital entitlements within Docked, not investments or ownership of a player, trademark or copyright. No particular player, resale value or financial return is guaranteed.

Requesting help or a refund
For a Google Play purchase issue, contact support@docked.com.au and use Google Play's purchase-support options. Include an order reference if available; do not send passwords or full payment details. Refunds and remedies remain subject to applicable consumer rights. Opening a pack does not by itself remove rights that cannot be excluded. We do not impose a blanket no-refunds rule.

Payment and refund safety
A pending or declined purchase does not provide spendable credits. The server verifies purchase state and the associated member before fulfilment. Repeated requests must not credit a purchase twice.

When Google confirms a refund or revocation, Docked reverses the remaining credits from that purchase once. If credits have already been spent, the account can enter a recovery hold: affected spending or transfers may be restricted while support reviews the purchase, spending and refund records. Your history remains available. A hold is not an automatic bank charge, a negative spendable balance, debt collection or permission to take another member's cards. It does not remove consumer-law remedies.

Contact support@docked.com.au to dispute a hold, correct a mistaken account association or provide relevant refund evidence. Support must verify the purchase and record the reason for any resolution. No automatic charge or unrelated-card clawback is authorised by this policy. TEST orders and commercial purchases remain separate.

Consumer safeguards
Unopened packs, completed openings and later card transfers are reviewed using the actual purchase evidence and applicable consumer law. There is no blanket forfeiture or no-refund rule. Opening a pack does not remove rights that cannot be excluded. Beta credits, cards and unopened packs do not become usable commercial assets. Their original ownership provenance, financial records and lifetime supply consumption remain preserved as historical records. Retirement does not waive statutory rights, reclaim serials or automatically clear a refund or recovery hold.

Measurement limits

and restricted-role SQL, simulated authenticated sessions, Docker stdin transport.

timing excludes durable COMMIT/fsync, hosted Auth and Internet latency. Separate
committed PostgreSQL acceptance establishes transaction integrity. Protected hosted
and genuine installed Android measurements remain required before promotion.

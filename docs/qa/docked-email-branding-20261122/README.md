# Docked authentication email branding — 22 November 2026

## Requested outcome

Make Docked the customer-facing brand and remove unnecessary references to Ginty United Investments, while preserving required operator disclosures. Add a branded verification-email signature.

## Scope

The Production authentication email is generated by the `docked-auth-email` Supabase Edge Function and delivered through the existing Microsoft Graph sender, `support@docked.com.au`. It is not the default Supabase dashboard email template.

The function's four original files were retrieved from Production version 50 and verified byte-for-byte against the parent source `4a562c54d5f920c4fbbecf547427cc989db86fed` on `codex/public-email-links-20261121`. The candidate is isolated on `codex/docked-email-branding-20261122`.

The new presentation uses the existing Docked icon and navy/blue palette, action-specific buttons, an identical fallback authentication URL, and this signature:

> The Docked team  
> support@docked.com.au  
> docked.com.au

A compact, readable footer retains the legal operator and ABN. It is separate from the prominent brand and support signature.

## Website and legal identity audit

The live homepage and `/app/signup` were retrieved successfully and contained no Ginty United reference. The current application-source references are confined to policy/operator content; the account-deletion page's operator identification is retained. Historical beta and legal evidence is not rewritten.

ASIC's company-identification guidance and Corporations Act ss 88A/153 cover company public documents and business correspondence, not only Terms and Privacy pages. The decision to retain a compact legal line on authentication correspondence applies that general rule; it is not an express regulator determination about this specific template.

- https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/companies/register-a-company/australian-company-number-acn
- https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00818/2026-09-19/2026-09-19/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html

## Google receipt boundary

Google documents a public merchant brand name and card-statement name separately from the payments profile's legal name. Its payments-profile documentation says the legal name is shown on customer receipts. The legal seller must not be replaced with a brand that is not its verified legal identity.

No Google account settings were changed. The connected local browser was unavailable. Automatic approval review rejected the cloud-browser Console navigation after it encountered an unexpected `google.play` destination. No alternative browser route was attempted after that rejection.

- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/7162811?hl=en

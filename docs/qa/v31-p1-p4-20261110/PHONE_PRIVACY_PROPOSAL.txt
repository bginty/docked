Public phone verification — narrow proposal, not published or activated
10 November 2026

Approved business requirement: one account per verified phone. No further
approval of that requirement is requested. The following implementation and
disclosure facts still require operational confirmation.

Proposed customer notice (add only after provider/retention review):

"We verify your phone number to help prevent duplicate accounts and abuse.
One verified phone number can be linked to one Docked account. We use our
authentication provider and its configured SMS delivery provider to send a
verification code. Your number is not displayed on your public profile and is
not used for marketing through this verification step. If you lose access to
your number, contact support@docked.com.au. You can still request account
deletion through your authenticated account controls. We keep necessary
account-security records only for the purposes and periods explained in our
retention policy."

This wording deliberately does not promise a deletion period that has not been
verified across Auth, the SMS provider and backups. The final policy must name
the actual provider/data flow where appropriate and state the approved period.
Do not alter existing consents retroactively. Publish a new prospective privacy
version and update Google Data Safety's phone-number collection declaration
before activating collection. No policy or Console change occurred here.

Actual preparation

- Supabase user-session updateUser(phone) and verifyOtp(phone_change), followed
  by a fresh authenticated user read; no admin confirmation or auth bypass.
- Confirmed Auth phone must be E.164 and unique. Existing database index reused.
- Public gameplay, onboarding and purchase access require this evidence.
- One number does not prove one human; purchased/recycled numbers remain an
  abuse/support risk. Do not advertise perfect identity verification or KYC.
- Numbers/codes are not written to localStorage, analytics or app audit logs.
  The audit stores account ID and a verification event only. Auth and the
  chosen SMS provider necessarily process the number; SMS is not anonymous.
- Same-origin bounded endpoint, 3 sends/account/hour, 6 verification attempts/
  account/hour, global 30 sends/hour. Public signup also has source/global
  quotas. Provider-side limits are independently necessary because the Auth
  publishable key exposes normal Auth endpoints; application quotas alone do
  not constrain direct Auth API use.
- Changes to an already verified number require support review. No automated
  duplicate-account merge, number reassignment or permanent denylist was added.
- Beta access and owner/admin MFA unchanged. Account export/delete remain
  available without completing this phone step for profile-backed accounts.

Observed hosted configuration

disableSignup=true; email autoconfirm=false; phone auth=false; phone
autoconfirm=false; configured provider identifier=twilio; OTP expiry=60s;
provider SMS rate limit=30/hour; CAPTCHA=false; leaked-password checking=false.
The provider identifier does not prove credentials, balance, country routing,
sender registration or external delivery. Zero phone-confirmed accounts.

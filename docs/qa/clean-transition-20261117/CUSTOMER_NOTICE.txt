DOCKED PRIVATE BETA — PROPOSED END-OF-BETA NOTICE
Draft for founder review and delivery before account retirement. Not yet sent.

Docked's paid Australian launch will be a fresh start for every customer.
Your private-beta account will be retired before public registration opens.
We will confirm the transition time before retiring your beta access. Until
then, do not assume public registration or paid features are available.

At public launch, eligible Australian adults can register themselves again
using their previous email address and Australian mobile number. You must
verify both again and accept the commercial Terms and Privacy notice yourself.
The new account will have a new identity. Its starting credits, cards, unopened
packs, active teams and marketplace assets will be zero, just like any new
customer's. Beta credits, cards, unopened packs, rankings, invitations,
discounts, social connections and other test advantages will not carry over.
An overseas private-beta exception does not provide Australian launch eligibility.

The two retained Owners keep their individual administrative identities and
MFA. They receive no usable commercial assets or payment exemptions from beta.

Your historical beta records are not being rewritten as commercial purchases.
Docked will preserve the required transaction, TEST receipt, refund, recovery,
consent, card issuance, serial and custody evidence with restricted operational
access under the applicable retention policy. Historical cards continue to
consume permanent lifetime supply; they will not be reminted or resold as
fresh inventory. An unopened beta pack will not be automatically opened.

The new account will not be able to retrieve the old account's assets merely
by reusing an email address or phone. Requests about your old records, privacy,
refund evidence or a specific entitlement can be made to support@docked.com.au.
Please raise any specific protected entitlement before the notified transition
so it can be reviewed. This notice does not remove non-excludable rights.

Do not send a password, authenticator code or SMS code to support.

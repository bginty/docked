Provider recommendation and exact founder setup
Use Supabase Auth with Twilio Programmable Messaging, matching the selected
provider and existing phone_change flow. Supabase also supports MessageBird,
Vonage and community-supported TextLocal. A different supplier would require
its own account and acceptance; a custom SMS hook adds code and secret handling.
Supabase generates/checks OTPs here; do not add Twilio Verify pricing or substitute
a standalone Verify-console test for acceptance of this integration. [1][2]

1. Sign into an existing founder-controlled Twilio account, if available; enable
   strong account MFA. If none exists, the founder must register/verify the account
   and review the funding quote/payment method directly in Twilio. Do not put
   credentials or the test mobile number in chat, Git or these reports.
2. Use a dedicated nonproduction subaccount/Messaging Service for this test if the
   account permits it. Choose an OTP/verification use case, SMS only. Authorise
   Australia destinations only; check fraud controls and disable automatic recharge.
3. Prefer the registered alphanumeric sender DOCKED, subject to evidence of brand
   entitlement and approval. Submit the sender through Twilio's Australian sender
   registration process; complete the ACMA/ABR confirmation requested of the founder.
   A Twilio-only historical registration is not proof of current ACMA status.
   Since July 2026 unregistered branded senders appear as Unverified. Twilio lists
   five business days for provisioning; actual review may take longer. [3][4]
4. Add the approved sender to the Messaging Service sender pool. Supply its
   Messaging Service SID (MG...), the matching Account SID (AC...), and that
   account/subaccount Auth Token directly to the isolated Supabase Phone provider
   settings. Store recovery copies only through the approved managed vault.
   These are server/provider credentials, never NEXT_PUBLIC variables or app code.
5. Operator validates the existing isolated project's identity and private hosting
   protection before configuration. Keep Auth Allow new users to sign up OFF and
   SMS auto-confirm OFF; leave the fixed-test-phone map empty. Proposed acceptance
   values: phone provider ON only in Preview, OTP six digits/300 seconds, resend
   interval at least 60 seconds, project SMS cap 6/hour. Restore phone OFF at close.
   Do not apply these changes to Production in this task.
6. Founder authorises the finite test plan/budget below and enters the mobile
   directly into the protected test form. Operator supplies two isolated, existing,
   email-verified ordinary test accounts with no genuine balances or staff roles.
   Verify their prior provisioning; do not use an admin phone-confirm operation.
7. Review the prospective phone disclosure/recovery terms below. Confirm actual
   provider data handling/retention before any public collection, then arrange the
   policy/Data Safety update and its review independently of the Android binary.

Expected Australian SMS cost and A$20 boundary
Public Twilio rate checked 10 November: US$0.0515/outbound segment; 20 single-
segment sends = US$1.03. Using a planning FX assumption of A$1.60/US$ (not a live
exchange-rate quote), this is A$1.65 before taxes/carrier fees. Reserve A$5 for
message usage within a total A$20 acceptance ceiling. Alphanumeric sender rental
is listed free; an AU mobile rental is US$8.25/month and is not proposed. [5]

Stop at 20 actual SMS segments or A$5 message usage, whichever occurs first;
never incur more than A$20 total new charge without further authority. Check
the invoice currency, GST, carrier fees, account funding and sender fees first.
An initial cash deposit is different from SMS consumption: a new account may
require a larger deposit (older official Twilio guidance quotes US$20 minimum).
Obtain the current Console quote; if it exceeds A$20, stop for that exact amount's
approval or use an existing funded account. No new payment has been authorised.
Twilio's current trial restricts custom SMS content, so its free units are not
assumed usable for Supabase-generated OTP bodies. Test credentials do not prove
real SMS delivery. A delayed usage-trigger webhook is an alert, not a hard cap.
Use a supervised short window, provider limits and stop controls. [6][7][8]

Hosted test preparation now completed
New optional DOCKED_ISOLATED_PHONE_TEST mode is bound to the existing protected
Preview project, Preview Auth and least-privilege database credentials. It
requires REGISTRATION_ENABLED=false, exactly two existing account UUIDs, a
maximum six-hour expiry, phone/policy flags and Google TEST checkout=false.
All inherited commerce/prize/retention/Production gates remain closed. Both
ordinary and invitation signup are denied by the shared auth admission check;
the customer-invitation route remains disabled outside beta. The phone command
admits only those two authenticated accounts. Ordinary public Preview mode no
longer sends OTPs without the explicit private phone test mode.

Prepare these server-only values in the protected Preview deployment only:
DOCKED_ISOLATED_PUBLIC=true
DOCKED_ISOLATED_PHONE_TEST=true
DOCKED_ISOLATED_PHONE_MEMBERS=<two existing fixture UUIDs, comma separated>
DOCKED_ISOLATED_PHONE_UNTIL=<UTC test end, no more than six hours ahead>
DOCKED_PUBLIC_CUSTOMERS=true
DOCKED_PUBLIC_PHONE_OTP=true
DOCKED_PUBLIC_PHONE_POLICY_REFERENCE=<approved private test notice reference>
REGISTRATION_ENABLED=false
DOCKED_ISOLATED_GOOGLE_TEST=false
Keep the established isolated project/origin/database allowlists, all commercial
switches false, and Supabase disable_signup=true. No values were set or deployed.
The name PUBLIC_CUSTOMERS selects the ordinary-member code path; it is not a
registration authorisation. Both Auth and application registration stay closed.
The account allowlist is an application control; provider Auth endpoints remain
directly reachable, so the provider cap/destination restrictions are mandatory.

Acceptance sequence (phone-test-plan.json is an unexecuted checklist)
A. Read back project/origin, closed signup and provider settings; establish baseline
   fixture account counts. Anonymous and third-account requests must be denied;
   signup with/without an old invitation must fail and create no account.
B. Founder enters their AU mobile privately on the physical device. Trigger one
   code; record opaque attempt ID, send/receive/verification times and delivery
   status only. Founder reads the actual SMS and enters the received code.
   Require authoritative Auth same-user/same-number/confirmation-time evidence;
   an HTTP success, Twilio queued/accepted result or fixed OTP is insufficient.
C. Test wrong code, expiry beyond 300 seconds, replay after success, resend before
   60 seconds, three sends/account/hour and six verifications/account/hour.
   Respect the provider cap: do not weaken limits to speed tests. Use the two
   fixture accounts across planned windows, with at most 20 total segments.
D. Attempt duplicate verification on the second account and simultaneous confirm;
   at most one account may bind that canonical number. Check no profile, wallet,
   cards or original ownership was duplicated or replaced. Reuse the already
   passed isolated PostgreSQL race proof; this adds actual hosted Auth behaviour.
E. Exercise direct Auth requests, not only the app, for cap enforcement, phone
   changes and duplicate binding. Observe normal failure when the test provider
   is disabled; never revoke a live Production token. Recheck login, account
   controls and deletion access while unverified. Keep actual deletion limited
   to disposable Preview fixtures under the existing erasure procedure.
F. Record final provider usage and fixture state; close phone admission/window,
   disable Preview phone provider, and retain redacted evidence. No SMS/OTP/phone
   screenshots, message bodies or raw request logs belong in the evidence pack.

Privacy and recovery changes still required
The latest detailed saved Data Safety evidence (5 November console-audit.json)
lists names/email/user IDs and other categories but not phone number. The
10 November handover says existing declarations were preserved. No fresh Console
inspection is claimed. Reconcile the actual saved form before publishing changes.
Google explicitly includes controlled WebView collection, so phone collection
must be declared even though no SMS-reading permission/native SDK is added. [9]

Proposed prospective notice (not published):
"We use your Australian mobile number to verify control of the number and help
prevent duplicate accounts and abuse. One verified number can be linked to one
Docked account. Supabase Auth stores the number and Twilio processes it to send
verification texts; overseas service processing may occur. We do not display
your number publicly or use this verification step for marketing. We retain the
current number while needed for your account and handle security records,
provider logs and backups under the published retention policy. If you lose
access to your number, contact support@docked.com.au. Account-deletion controls
remain available without completing phone verification."

Before approval, add the chosen provider, actual processing locations/contracts,
required collection/purposes (account management, functionality, fraud prevention),
service-provider sharing assessment, deletion and backup periods to the policy
and Data Safety form. The 90-day operational-incident policy does not govern Auth
phone records or SMS logs. Proposed provider-record access period: seven days,
disable indefinite archive retention where available; verify the provider's
actual deletion/backup terms. Twilio's default customer-facing records can persist
400 days and older archives are not deleted by default. Optional immediate
redaction can require paid Editions, and redaction is not full erasure. Do not
promise immediate deletion or purchase an add-on under the A$20 test budget. [10][11]

Existing beta users keep beta access; no accounts are auto-migrated or confirmed.
After eventual public activation, existing users must complete verification for
public gameplay/commerce. One phone is not proof of one human or AU residency.
Keep verified email/password recovery and staff AAL2 independently available.
For a lost/recycled number, require established account authentication/evidence,
fresh verification of the replacement and review of any conflicting account.
Do not infer ownership from possession of a recycled number, auto-merge accounts,
move cards/balances, or set phone_confirm=true administratively. Revoke old
sessions after a verified security recovery. A direct Auth number change may
bypass the UI's support-only change message, so acceptance must establish this
behaviour before advertising support approval as an enforced provider control.
Maintain the one-current-verified-number constraint without a hidden permanent
phone blacklist; deletion/re-registration handling needs the precise published
recovery/retention policy. Genuine hosted recovery and OTP remain unaccepted.

v31 compatibility assessment
Static archive inspection confirms the AAB SHA-256 exactly matches the submitted
artifact: 8becb6d044efc93baed6649f11ff100cc562bb09d200d45ec1a6742cf36e83b0.
Both AAB/APK pin https://docked.com.au/app, package docked.fantasy, HTTPS only,
logging/debug inspection disabled. No android/, Capacitor configuration or
package/lockfile change exists between Production f1b50b6f and 8acae031.
Evidence: android-compatibility.json. The new changes are web/server/SQL only;
manual OTP entry needs no SMS permission or native bridge change. Existing
BillingPlugin/image-sharing behaviour and the two credit product IDs are intact.

Therefore the same v31 binary can consume the approved backend after a later
deployment. Registration is controlled by application environment, database
registration authority and Supabase signup settings; it does not need a new
Android release. These independent controls must be approved and aligned;
setting DOCKED_PUBLIC_PHONE_OTP alone does not open registration.

The submitted binary cannot point at isolated Preview. A physical phone browser
test against Preview is useful but is not installed-v31 WebView acceptance.
The exact installed-v31 OTP/navigation smoke test follows the separately approved
dormant web deployment/closed hosted acceptance configuration. No certificate,
hosts-file or origin substitution should be used to manufacture that evidence.

Supabase's 10 November CBC TLS deprecation affects old HTTP API clients, not
Postgres/poolers/Management API. No custom CBC-only TLS configuration was found
in the Android source; minSDK 24 and normal WebView/HTTPS are used. There is no
established native blocker, but the planned current-device HTTPS/Auth test remains
the practical compatibility check; today's management GETs do not prove it. [12]

Commercial/payment compatibility remains conditional on the existing launch
gates: Google Play Billing for credit purchases, unchanged A$49/4,900 and
A$99/9,900 products, and displayed fixed pack odds before purchase. Backend
lifetime guards change enforcement, not advertised odds or native payment
contracts. Google requires Play billing for covered digital goods and nearby
odds disclosure for purchased random items. Do not replace this with external
payment links. Existing TEST evidence does not establish live settlement. [13]
The saved listing advertises paid credits/packs: publishing a billing-disabled
public product without reviewing listing accuracy is not accepted. Trading,
prizes, withdrawals, real scored competitions and NBA remain out of scope/off.

No Google status was refreshed or changed; v31 review/managed publishing ON are
the supplied state plus existing evidence, not newly independently observed.
Privacy/Data Safety changes may themselves require review. No binary bump is
needed solely for those forms, and no guarantee of Google's review timing is made.

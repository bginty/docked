Docked Social refinement — 8 November 2026

Scope and findings
The existing Social feed remains cross-sport. Its permission, privacy, moderation,
post-submission and inventory-fetch contracts are unchanged. The feed composer
occupied 241–259 px before typing. At 320 px, the Announcements tab clipped.
Opening Attach cards mounted every owned card: 180 cards and 19,448 descendant
DOM nodes in the isolated collection fixture. Inventory already loaded lazily;
there was no redundant initial ownership request to remove.

Implemented
- Social Home has a compact What's happening? prompt and directly accessible
  Attach cards / Add image actions. Opening it exposes the existing post form.
- Minimise returns keyboard focus to Continue your draft and keeps the current
  text, attachments and options in component memory. A failed post preserves them.
- The compact mode is opt-in on Social Home only. Share My Pull and the separate
  compose screen retain their existing expanded behaviour.
- Header actions wrap, and Latest / Following / Announcements fit narrow screens.
- The attachment picker mounts at most 24 cards, with accessible page controls.
  Search, same-sport attachment restrictions and the four-card limit remain.
  The first selection keeps its card on the visible page after sport filtering.
  Deselecting the last card restores its global page and keyboard focus as the
  list expands back to all sports (covered by the global-index-25 AFL case).
  Page changes reset the card-grid scroll position; draft selections survive.
- Selected-card and search filtering are memoized independently of body typing.

Samsung dirty-composer follow-up
Physical review found attachment labels painting outside their buttons once
Cancel appeared. The two flexible form columns were being squeezed by the
auto-sized Post/Cancel column. The document itself did not overflow, so the
earlier document-width assertion did not cover this defect.
Dirty mobile forms now use two attachment columns followed by an equal-width
Post/Cancel row. The existing blue Post action fills its column. Clean three-
button forms and the collapsed composer retain their layout and behaviour.

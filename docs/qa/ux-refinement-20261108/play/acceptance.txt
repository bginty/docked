Play / round presentation refinement — 2026-11-08

Scope and outcome
The existing navy/blue styling, lineup cards, scoring, salaries, mutations and lock rules are preserved. NFL/AFL/NRL competition navigation now consistently says Round; basketball retains Week. Private season IDs remain persisted unchanged. Ordinary selectors use a short round label, with Practice/Test as secondary context and season only where needed to distinguish duplicate options.

The oversized NFL week modal has been replaced with a compact device-native selector. The same selector is used by the existing sport sandbox Play surface. Previous/next remain 44px targets. A wrapping visible label avoids native select text truncation at enlarged text sizes while the native select retains keyboard/screen-reader/device picker behaviour. Draft-change confirmation, URL navigation and the caller's selected competition remain owned by the existing components.

Statuses use supplied competition timestamps: explicit scored_at = Final; a past lock without finality = Entries closed; future opens_at = Upcoming; otherwise valid opening time = Entries open. Missing opening time is conservatively Open for planning. Statistics availability alone does not open a round or label it final. Actual Production NFL6/7 are not marked Final from their past deadlines. No Round8 record was invented or created.

Rankings and Prizes are paired compact links on NFL Play, including when no entry exists. The NFL Game Day/Rankings/Prizes wording is Round. Historical results, query parameter names and scoring structures are unchanged. At320px the existing prize thumbnail squeezed Standard into broken word fragments: reward text now wraps beneath the unchanged thumbnail when needed.

DOCKED FINISHED FOIL PACKS — OWNER DESIGN REVIEW
7 November 2026 — local preview only, not deployed

Requested finished products
- Standard, AFL, 9 cards: electric blue metallic wrapper.
- Premium, NRL, 9 cards: black/obsidian and metallic gold wrapper.
- Original Docked wordmark supplied as the branding reference.
- DOCKED, family, sport and quantity are baked into each finished bitmap.
  No HTML lettering is placed over the pack. Accessible product labels remain
  ordinary UI text beneath it. Only ambient lighting and tilt are dynamic.
- The earlier blue and black/gold renders were unbranded base assets.

Customer interface evidence
- Real AppShell, sport selector, PackShop, purchase review and existing card
  reveal components, rendered with local synthetic read-only data.
- The fixture substitutes the two artwork variants without modifying the
  production PackArtwork component or any production route.
- Connected Chrome captures: chrome-afl-store.jpg, chrome-nrl-store.jpg,
  chrome-afl-enlarged.jpg and chrome-nrl-enlarged.jpg.
- Contact sheets reproduce those captures without retouching or cropping.
- Enlarged preview includes a short split-seal/light/fan transition into the
  existing Legendary card reveal. This is a lightweight 2.5D prototype, not
  a physical foil simulation. Sample cards do not represent pack probabilities.
- Local preview rejects non-GET requests and has no customer credentials,
  production database connection, purchase callback or inventory mutation.

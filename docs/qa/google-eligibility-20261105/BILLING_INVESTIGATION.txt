Acknowledgement correction

dispatched the legacy TEST queue, not the commercial queue. Prompt automatic
consumption was therefore missing. The server patch 7c8b0574 now claims only
the authenticated member/receipt's existing consume job immediately after
credit commit. Provider failure leaves the retry job and credit audit intact.
Authenticated notifications persist first, then dispatch bounded background
work. The existing cron also dispatches a configured commercial service,

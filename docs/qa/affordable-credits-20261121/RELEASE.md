## Customer economics

At the existing rate, an empty wallet is 100 credits short of Premium 9 after buying 4,900. The UI shows the cost, cheapest available combination and residual credits based on the actual Play offers and the available wallet. With all four tiers, A$19 + A$19 + A$7 + A$7 totals A$52, leaving 200. A$49 + A$7 uses two checkouts and leaves 600. A future A$1 option could close the exact 100-credit gap. Do not advertise an A$50 exact path until it exists. An exact A$50 / 5,000 product would simplify this to one checkout and requires its own decision.

Optional A$1 / 100 and A$3 / 300 would fund a Standard one/three card pack. With GST of gross/11 and a 15%, 25% or 30% effective Play fee on the ex-GST amount, indicative net per order is A$0.77 / 0.68 / 0.64 at A$1 and A$2.32 / 2.05 / 1.91 at A$3. At A$7 it is A$5.41 / 4.77 / 4.45. No separate fixed card-processing charge has been evidenced. These are scenarios before refunds, order rounding, support and infrastructure costs. Confirm the account's actual enrolled tier and settlement statements before budgeting margin. Google lists AU's new schedule from 30 September 2026, with separate billing and service fee components and eligibility rules. Recommend launching the two requested tiers first. Track failed/returned orders and support cost per sale before deciding on micro-top-ups.

# Australian fixed-price Marketplace decision

## Existing commercial code and change

The public /fantasy/market page always returned a closed notice. This candidate renders the existing Marketplace only when the public commercial service, surfaces and Marketplace flags admit it. memberMarketplace selects the commercial adapter. Its command and query paths recheck the server environment and database transfers_enabled. Administrator reporting directs the Owner to Commercial operations; no beta administration role is granted.

The commercial market ledger, view and November 13 request-guard migration already implement listing, reprice, cancel, private sale receipts and atomic ownership/credit transfer. Runtime rights do not grant direct public finance writes. The sale review binds buyer, listing revision, card, price, seller fee, net and expiry. Both sides must retain valid eligibility and clear financial holds at settlement. Replay uses the recorded buyer/request reference. The sale moves the existing card and its serial; it creates no new issuance. Retired, restricted, unconfirmed, out-of-region and blocked relationships fail existing identity and party checks. Customer restrictions and global transfer closure must remain available to operations.

The November commercial schedule deducts 5% from seller proceeds, rounded half up to whole credits, capped at 2,000 with no minimum. Buyer pays the listed price. October private beta evidence used 7.5% for sales, and max(7.5%, swap quote) for mixed offers. Those are different historical schedules. The later published commercial policy draft and commercial implementation agree on 5%; use the exact 5% schedule in any approval and customer notices. Do not enable beta settlement.

## Google and disclosure scope

The live Play Console showed no policy issues, 10 actioned declarations and AU Mature (M). The November rating includes digital purchases with random items and online interaction. Docked previously represented the first release with trading disabled. Neither the score nor the absence of an enforcement issue establishes acceptance of customer-to-customer resale.

The published Australian Data Safety form declares collection of User IDs, purchase history, in-app interactions and optional user-generated content. It lists phone numbers, diagnostics and device/other IDs under sharing. Review precisely which member display name/handle, asking price, listing activity and receipts are visible to a counterparty. Check Google's intentional user-initiated transfer exception, listing preview and consent against actual recipients. Do not declare financial records publicly shared if they remain private. Update actual changed answers or listing claims before going live. Re-submit the rating questionnaire if this feature changes any submitted answer. WebView functionality counts. A v31-compatible web route needs no native change by itself. A definitive no-review decision is premature; the top-up binary separately requires Google review.

Google requires Play Billing for in-app currency and confines it to one title. Its Payments policy excludes peer-to-peer payments and online auctions from Play Billing. Docked offers a fixed-price collectible for previously purchased in-app credits; the seller receives respendable credits, Docked retains commission and no cash redemption or external payment exists. The published text does not decide this second-leg question for Docked. Obtain the narrower written determination before enabling transfers. Confirm Australian advice on transferable paid credits and paid random cards covers unrelated member sellers; the player-name and artwork advice concerns another question.

Can we enable this on the existing approved binary and what changes, if any, are required to our declarations, rating questionnaire or store submission before enabling it? ## Phase 2

Use the existing private offers code as design evidence. Keep commercial command schemas fixed-price only. A commercial swap/counteroffer needs immutable proposed card IDs, versions, participants, exact fee, expiry and parent offer ID; only one proposal can settle. Reprice, ownership transfer, listing, lineup lock, account restriction, block or recovery hold must invalidate conflicting offers. Validate ownership under the same transaction and lock parties and card IDs in deterministic order. Accept once, preserve both custody histories, record cancellation/expiry, and deny cash equivalents and credit gifts. Freeze the commercial swap/mixed fee and approve those routes independently before implementation is activated.

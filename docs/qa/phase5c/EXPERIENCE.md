# Phase 5C research interface evidence

The new research interface uses the existing five-tab shell, private staff MFA boundary and authenticated community projection. These focused checks are local, clearly labelled DEMO presentation tests; they do not prove hosted database authorization or supplier availability.

## Implemented surface

- `/admin/research`: canonical upcoming matches, versioned sources and permission reviews, actual request-ledger counts, nullable source clocks, feature governance, schedules/jobs and editorial drafts.
- `/admin/research/[eventId]`: exact immutable snapshot selection, missing sections, confidence/conflicts, source attribution and observation timeline. Manual facts, snapshots, editorial drafts, publication review and recalculation eligibility use typed structured forms. No probability override exists.
- `/research/matches/[contentId]`: reviewed factual content only, with current source display rights and member jurisdiction checks performed by the server. Feed cards and watchlist links never become official Edges or alter performance records.
- Research, lineup and future team-update notification preferences are separate opt-ins. No team follow is invented and no email/push transport is enabled.
- Native links allow only UUID member research content on the approved preview origin. Admin paths, arbitrary event IDs, queries and foreign hosts remain rejected.

Unknown metrics remain `Unknown` or `Data not available`. The model boundary says `MODEL BLOCKED — DATA REQUIRED`. Source governance starts at `REVIEW_REQUIRED` with unknown permissions; saving a review never itself enables acquisition. Derived sporting evidence and market comparison remain separate.

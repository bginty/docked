# Lineup and availability research — Phase 5C

PLAYER_INJURY, PLAYER_SUSPENSION, PLAYER_RETURN, EXPECTED_LINEUP and CONFIRMED_LINEUP are distinct structured evidence types. They require mapped teams; player facts also require mapped players. A confirmed lineup requires eleven unique mapped player IDs and CONFIRMED evidence. Expected lineups remain expected, even when a source is reliable. Unconfirmed-tier sources cannot claim confirmation.

The evidence envelope retains source/version/item/revision, public evidence reference/hash, publication time when actually known, source observation and ingestion times, effective time, expiry and correction ancestry. Unknown upstream publication time is null; downloading a page does not fabricate its publication date. As-of views exclude future observations, ingestion and effective evidence. Licence retention and expiry can make a payload unavailable while permitted provenance hashes remain auditable.

Corrections append a successor plus reason, preserving the original. Withdrawal does not resurrect an earlier assertion. Broken or branched correction ancestry fails closed. Identical cross-source assertions share one factual grain while retaining corroboration IDs; conflicting values stay CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE. Confirmed official evidence may be preferred for display, but a disagreement is still visible and is excluded from automatic model eligibility. Official provenance alone does not grant storage, public display or automation permission.

The resolver can use immutable ledger ancestry metadata after licensed payload expiry: fact ID/key, event/source, ingestion/effective clocks and predecessor link. This contains no fact value or article content. It prevents an expired withdrawal from reviving an older assertion and permits a new licensed correction to retain its lawful hash-only lineage. Metadata is never enough to display a fact or enter a model; the chosen assertion still needs a current authorised retained payload. Captured historical evidence sets remain distinct from current complete-event resolution.

Lineup drafts use the explicit LINEUP_UPDATE type and DRAFT_REQUIRES_REVIEW status. The public projection contains only permitted structured values, attribution and observation clocks; private contracts, evidence hashes and raw payloads are not included. No automatic content publication, notification sending or player-impact estimate follows from a lineup update. Richer research remains useful context while the independent model is unavailable.

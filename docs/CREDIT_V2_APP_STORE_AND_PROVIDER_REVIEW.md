Reviewed 13 October 2026. These are official-source findings and questions, not a
legal opinion, merchant approval or permission to activate money. No enquiry sent.
No subscription/account created. Free, nonredeemable invited beta stays separate.

## Product submitted for classification

AU adults; NFL initially. Random nine-player digital-card packs; no NFTs. Credits
cost A$1 per 100. Cards have permanent scarcity, resale and 7.5% seller commission.
Proposed eligible seller proceeds can cash out to AUD. Free fantasy entry; no
monetary prizes. New opt-in competitions apply card-rarity scoring multipliers.
Unused-credit cash-out undecided. Existing test grants have no cash value.

Additional official checks (13 October 2026): [PayPal Payouts AU agreement](https://www.paypal.com/au/legalhub/paypal/paypal-payouts-agreement)
requires a business account in good standing and permission for Payouts;
[platform onboarding](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/get-started) requires
business evaluation. This does not establish a direct-bank payout rail for Docked.
[ACMA's Interactive Gambling Act explanation](https://www.acma.gov.au/about-interactive-gambling-act)
describes prohibited online services. Its [submission hosted by ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/australian-communications-media-authority-submission-dpsi.pdf?download=y)
states classification depends on the service's features and distinguishes games not
played for money/value. Docked's proposed cash-out feature therefore needs specific
assessment; ordinary nonredeemable loot-box treatment cannot simply be assumed.

above for an Australian operator and AU18+ customers. Specifically:
1. Are first-party random digital-card packs, a reloadable credit wallet, third-party
   card resale and seller AUD redemption accepted together? Which AUP approvals apply?
2. Can the same funded credits circulate between sellers? Who legally holds customer

3. Are payouts to verified Australian bank accounts supported for these proceeds?
   Does unused-credit refund differ from withdrawal? What settlement, reserve,
   dispute, chargeback, beneficiary and reconciliation APIs are required?
4. Provide exact AU pricing, minimums, reserve/holding requirements and contractual
   restrictions. Do not activate anything; this is an eligibility enquiry.

and quotation before paid work. No legal engagement is authorised by this draft.

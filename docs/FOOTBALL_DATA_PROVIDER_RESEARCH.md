# Football sporting-data provider research

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against public first-party documentation. No account, subscription, API call, dataset download or purchase was made for this review. Prices below are advertised prices, not a Docked invoice or confirmed account entitlement. Exchange conversion, tax and rightsholder fees are not estimated.

**Decision: INSUFFICIENT_AUTHORISED_DATA.** No provider is configured for independent football modelling or automatic football settlement. The existing Odds API trial supplies market observations, not the required sporting training history. A reachable free endpoint does not establish commercial modelling, public display or permanent evidence-retention rights.

## Recommended next decision

Investigate football-data.org first for a genuinely free EPL/La Liga sporting-data option, but ask for written permission covering the cancellation conflict before importing. API-Football is the other free technical candidate, with a substantial publication-rights gap. If free rights/history are insufficient, return to the owner with the Sportmonks Starter cost and exact scope; do not start a card-backed trial or upgrade automatically.

No primary/fallback **operational selection** is approved. The gate stays closed until both legal scope and a coverage manifest establish sufficient data. Optional injuries, lineups and xG must not turn into mandatory paid dependencies for the initial goals baseline.

The exact questions for the selected provider are: may Docked ingest once and store canonical regulation results; train/version an independent sporting model; display derived probabilities in a commercial community product; retain prediction inputs, result revisions and derived audit evidence indefinitely (including after cancellation); and publish limited final results with attribution? Ask which EPL/La Liga seasons and promoted-team histories are included, whether historical corrections carry effective/observation timestamps, what deletion obligations apply, and whether separate competition permissions are needed.

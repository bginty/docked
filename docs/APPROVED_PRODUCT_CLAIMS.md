## Claim approval record requirements

A future claim is not approved until all fields below are complete:

1. Claim ID and exact customer-facing wording.
2. Final product title, supplier model, SKU and variant scope.
3. Evidence file/reference, issuer, date, report number and tested sample.
4. Any qualification, measurement method, permitted context and expiry/retest date.
5. Product-safety/compliance reviewer and review date.
6. Legal/marketing review where the claim could affect safety, price or consumer expectations.
7. Matching wording in the final manual, label and packaging.
8. Approved display locations and translations.
9. Owner approval and date.
10. Recheck after any material product, supplier, component, packaging or copy change.

Safety copy is maintained separately in `docs/SAFETY_COPY_REGISTER.md`. Draft safety wording is not an approved performance claim and does not replace product testing, markings, packaging, instructions, insurance or legal obligations.

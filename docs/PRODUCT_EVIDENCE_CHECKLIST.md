It records what must be obtained; it does not certify the product, approve a claim, or decide whether a standard applies. Supplier component files received on 15 August 2026 remain under review. They do not approve a final production model/SKU or permit Active status; DC-02 must remain Draft until all gates in [SKU approval workflow](SKU_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md) pass.

## Evidence acceptance rules

Evidence is acceptable only when it:

- identifies the supplier, manufacturer, exact model, production revision and SKU;
- applies to the final product, components, packaging and instructions that Docked will actually supply;
- is complete, legible, dated and attributable to its issuer;
- includes a laboratory name, report number, tested sample identification, methods, results and authorised signatory where testing is relied on;
- can be reconciled to the product label, manual, packaging, Shopify data and customer-facing claims;
- has been checked for scope, expiry, retest or surveillance requirements; and
- is stored in an access-controlled evidence repository, with only a safe reference committed to Git.

A supplier statement such as “adult product”, a CE/FCC mark, a marketplace listing, a sample photo or a certificate for a similar model is not sufficient by itself. Redact banking details, credentials, personal data and commercially sensitive material before placing anything in the repository.

## Category-specific overlays

### Canopy products

- Canopy attachment, release/folding and wind-use instructions.
- Evidence supporting any sun-protection claim; otherwise no quantified or complete-protection claim.
- Confirmation that the canopy is not presented as a grab handle.

### Pool games, floating coolers and drink holders

- Final pool-size, water-depth, capacity and anchoring requirements.
- Net, cord and anchor entanglement assessment for games.
- Verified load/capacity and stability evidence for coolers and trays.
- Packaging and imagery that exclude glass and do not promote intoxication around a pool.

### Pumps, repair products and storage pouch

- Pump ratings, instructions, overinflation controls and product compatibility.
- For the rechargeable pump: battery, electrical, charging and transport evidence above.
- Repair-kit ingredient/material declaration, safety data where applicable, application limits, warnings and compatibility evidence.
- Storage-pouch test report, test method, rating, closure/use instructions and exclusions before any waterproof or water-resistant claim.

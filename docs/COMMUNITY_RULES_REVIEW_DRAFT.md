# Proposed community rules and moderation operations

Prepared 3 October 2026 for owner review. **This is an unpublished proposal, not
accepted member terms, a staffed moderation promise or legal/Play approval.** The
existing application has reporting, blocking, muting and audited staff controls;
their presence does not establish an operating moderation service. No terms or
consent version is changed by this document.

## Proposed member rules

The following applies to profiles, names, bios, posts, comments, images and links.
Members must be adults meeting the applicable access policy. Preview permission
is temporary test access and does not establish regional approval for live Edges.

- No threats, stalking, bullying, targeted harassment, hate or dehumanisation
  directed at people or protected groups. Do not encourage violence or abuse.
- No sexually explicit content, sexual services or exploitation. No sexualised
  depictions of minors, grooming, child abuse material or content endangering
  children. The proposed community does not permit an incidental-adult-content
  category. Do not invite or encourage minors to gamble.
- No scams, phishing, account theft, malicious links, requests for passwords,
  identity documents, payment access or sportsbook credentials. Do not expose
  someone else's private details, account identifiers or confidential material.
- No spam, repeated unsolicited promotions, undisclosed commercial relationships
  or affiliate/referral solicitation. Bookmaker promotions, paid analysis,
  competitions and prizes remain disabled unless separately approved for the
  relevant product and jurisdiction; member posts cannot bypass those controls.
- No impersonation of Docked, staff, another member or an organisation. Do not
  create misleading official badges or identities. Clearly labelled authorised
  preview test profiles are permitted only within their testing scope.
- No fabricated results, altered evidence, false verification claims or
  manipulation of a permanent performance record. Do not present synthetic
  prices, promotional prices or personal claims as standard verified prices.
  Label opinions and uncertainty; do not promise profit, encourage chasing losses
  or imply that a small winning sample establishes predictive skill.
- Post only material you have the right to share. Remove private information
  from images. A screenshot is social content, not proof of a verified price or
  result. Do not evade moderation, create replacement accounts to bypass a
  restriction, or abuse reporting tools.

Ordinary disagreement about sport or analysis is permitted when respectful.
Critique evidence and reasoning. Moderation must not selectively remove losses
or alter immutable accounting; factual corrections use the audited correction
process. Privacy and deletion requests must be handled through the disclosed
account and retention process.

## Member controls and proposed notice

Members can report a post, comment or profile from its controls. Reports are
private review requests; submitting one does not automatically prove wrongdoing
or erase a permanent Edge. Members can block another account to restrict social
interaction, or mute content to change what they see. Blocking and privacy do
not rewrite canonical performance records. Preview records remain excluded from
genuine rankings regardless of moderation or deletion.

Proposed enforcement options include removal of social commentary, rejection of
media, a warning, restricted posting or account suspension. Staff actions must
record the actor, reason and scope in the existing audit trail. Decisions should
consider severity, credible evidence, context and repeated conduct, rather than
popularity, paid membership, follower count or winning performance.

If adopted, members must be told how to appeal and how to request privacy review.
The current beta organiser's private invitation channel is not a verified public
appeals/support contact and must not be represented as one in a Play submission.

## Adoption gate

1. Owner and appropriate legal/policy reviewers approve the final rules,
   prohibited categories, support contacts, retention and operating plan.
2. Publish a stable member-facing version and record its version/date. Update the
   app's terms/consent flow so members accept that version before creating or
   uploading community content. An old generic terms checkbox is not evidence of
   acceptance of this new draft. Keep past consent history accurate.
3. Assign and verify the staff roles, coverage and escalation arrangements; test
   real report intake, action, notice and appeal end to end on labelled test
   content. Keep moderator credentials out of public review materials.
4. Reconcile the actual app, privacy policy, Data Safety, audience/rating and Play
   declarations. Provide reviewers with truthful scoped access and disclose
   preview restrictions. Submission still requires the other documented gates.

Google's current UGC policy calls for terms defining/prohibiting objectionable
content and ongoing moderation, with reporting/blocking appropriate to the
product. This proposal is preparation toward those requirements, not a finding
of compliance. [Official Google Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en).

Established cause

The current beta runtime is intentionally different from public customer mode.
Live GET https://docked.com.au/terms returned HTTP 200 after a redirect to
https://docked.com.au/beta-policies#terms. That does not prove public Terms are

The non-beta Production consent resolver separately requires an entity check
and explicit TERMS_VERSION and PRIVACY_POLICY_VERSION values. It refuses unset
or draft/preview/pending identifiers. Enabling a presentation flag or relabelling
the beta Terms cannot satisfy these gates. The SQL public_gameplay.policy must
bind the same versions, valid authority and unexpired eligibility; an active
region policy and explicit member consent are also enforced. No RLS bypass or
beta allowlist is needed or appropriate for ordinary public users.

NARROW PRIVACY CLARIFICATION — READY FOR OWNER REVIEW, NOT PUBLISHED

The new evidence is automatic diagnostic traffic in the existing signed v30 Google
Billing SDK. It does not change the commercial model, prices, gameplay or payment
availability. No replacement approval of those decisions is requested.

Exact proposed wording, in the existing Device information and analytics section:

The app has no advertising SDK. Android and Google Play may provide operating-system
diagnostics under their own settings.

The Android app also includes Google Play Billing Library. When the library is
initialised or used, it sends billing-operation results, technical app/device
information and a per-session identifier to Google for billing reliability,
performance improvement and error support. This automatic SDK processing can occur
without a completed purchase. It is separate from Docked's optional usage analytics
and is not disabled by that analytics preference. Google handles this information
under its own privacy terms.

Optional media selection and notification preferences follow device permissions
and application controls. Contacts, microphone and location access are not required
to buy a pack. We do not use identity documents or purchase tokens in Docked's
optional usage analytics.

The existing first paragraph of that section is unchanged. No other privacy
section or commercial/refund/retention wording changes.

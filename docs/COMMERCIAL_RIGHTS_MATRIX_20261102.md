## Australian and Google release assessment

- **Classification:** the [Australian Classification guidance](https://www.classification.gov.au/about-us/media-and-news/news/new-classifications-for-gambling-content-video-games) sets a minimum M category for purchases linked to chance and R18+ for simulated gambling. Paid random packs require accurate disclosure in IARC. Selecting an 18+ target audience is not a classification certificate. Confirm the actual Australian rating returned for the paid-pack configuration and preserve its certificate/reference.
- **Gambling/secondary value:** [ACMA's submission](https://www.acma.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-12/Sub-96-Australian-Communications-and-Media-Authority.pdf) distinguishes ordinary loot boxes from games played for money/value and specifically notes secondary-market uncertainty. Free entry and disabled cash/prizes materially reduce the issue; they do not prove that transferable paid cards have no value. Obtain a documented Australian assessment covering both Commonwealth and relevant state/territory rules for this model. Do not label random packs automatically illegal or automatically exempt.
- **Platform:** [Google Payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) requires Play Billing for applicable digital purchases and odds close to purchase. [Real-money games policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032) must be assessed against the actual linked cards, contests and transfers. Proposed safest first public configuration: purchased credits usable only in Docked; free Core/free no-prize play; no cash redemption, paid entry, prizes or commercial transfers. Existing beta sales remain separate. That proposal does not change the owner's approved 5% capped fee for any future permitted marketplace.
- **Consumer rights:** [ACCC remedies](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought) cannot be excluded by opening a pack or spending credits. Recovery holds must be reviewable and proportionate, with no automatic debt collection or unrelated-card confiscation. The refund draft preserves these limits.
- **Records/privacy:** [ASIC](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-business-and-companies/companies/company-building-blocks/company-record-keeping) requires company financial records for at least seven years; this does not justify keeping every profile, token or image for seven years. Apply data-category minimisation and, where applicable, [APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information). Accountant/privacy approval must identify records, retention trigger and exceptions, then an audited archive job can implement them.

EXACT PROVISIONAL RULESETS
All weights are independent of rarity, level, price, spend and ownership history.
Daily/engagement/collecting rewards are separate. No mixed-sport ranking or captain
multiplier, no subjective bonus/rating, no imported FPL final points.

docked-epl-beta-v1 / Docked EPL Scoring v1:
Any actual playing time below 60 regulation minutes: 1 appearance point. At least
60 regulation minutes: 2 total, not 1+2. Added time counts for actual participation
and all events but not the 60-minute threshold. A stoppage-time-only substitute gets
1. Regulation duration is capped at 90 minutes; EPL league fixtures have no extra time.
Goals GK/DEF6 MID5 FWD4; assist3; clean sheet GK/DEF4 MID1 FWD0, requiring >=60
regulation minutes, zero concessions during verified on-pitch intervals, no dismissal.
GK saves1 per complete3; penalty save5 in addition (penalty save included in saves).
Penalty miss-2; yellow-1; straight red-3 plus any earlier single yellow; own goal-2.
Second-yellow dismissal replaces BOTH yellow penalties with one -3 dismissal penalty.
GK/DEF conceded-1 per complete2 on-pitch goals against, including own goals.
No post-substitution/dismissal concessions counted. Substituted players can retain
clean-sheet points; dismissed players cannot. These are explicit Docked choices,
not exact FPL compatibility. Equal-minute events require reliable ordering, not a
guess about who was on the pitch. The interval adapter excludes events at the exact
entry/exit order; adapters must use distinct ordered event positions.
Synthetic assist definition: final deliberate teammate touch directly creating the
goal; no own-goal/rebound/material-deflection assist. No real provider is selected;
its documented assist definition must be reviewed and frozen before live activation.
Formation retained for the new synthetic EPL format: 1GK/4DEF/4MID/2FWD, 11 active.

docked-nfl-half-ppr-beta-v1 / Docked NFL Scoring v1:
Half-PPR explicitly selected: reception0.5. Passing yard0.04, passing TD4,
interception thrown-2. Rushing/receiving yard0.1 and TD6. Fumble lost-2 across all
phases once. Passing/rushing/receiving two-point conversion2 to the credited player.
Individual kickoff/punt return TD6 and offensive fumble recovery TD6. No return-yard

Overtime counts fully. Seven active: QB, 2RB, 2WR, TE, FLEX(RB/WR/TE). Existing NFL

Docked intentionally chooses half-PPR rather than that document's one-point reception.

LIFECYCLE / FAIRNESS
Sport-specific gameweek/week/round is metadata, not a shared clock assumption.
Each period freezes rules, fixture membership, positions and published UTC deadline
before submission. All slots lock together no later than earliest kickoff. Ownership,
eligibility and position are rechecked at lock; a failed check blocks scoring, never
silently replaces cards. Subsequent trades/position changes cannot change the snapshot.
Double fixtures each score once. No automatic transfers between periods. Postponed,
abandoned, cancelled, rescheduled or incomplete fixtures stay pending until resolved.
A resumption/replay must map to the original canonical fixture and replace old input;
no double-counting of abandoned and replay stats. Unresolved cancellation cannot be
finalised until a separate void policy is approved; no default final zero.
Known partial subtotals are labelled and standings withheld if any team is incomplete.
Byes need explicit provider evidence and a mapped period record; no missing-row inference.

Provisional windows: EPL72h, NFL96h, AFL72h from completion. Period finalisation waits
for all fixtures, player rows and elapsed windows; reviewer only. NFL96h is a beta
choice; it does not guarantee Thursday corrections always arrive before the window.
After finalisation or expiry, correction requires reviewer+reason, keeps all old
sources/calculations and reopens provisional results for reviewed finalisation.
The first completion timestamp anchors ordinary correction authority; changing a
later revision's end time cannot silently extend importer permissions.
Equal points share rank (competition ranking), and rankings remain within one sport.

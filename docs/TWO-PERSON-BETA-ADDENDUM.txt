This private test is limited to the existing owner and one invited Australian adult
aged 18 or older. Registration remains invitation-only and closed to everyone else.

no monetary value and cannot be deposited, withdrawn, redeemed or sold for money.
There are no cash prizes, real payments or validated official betting recommendations.

The two admitted accounts may exchange beta cards through the sandbox swap flow.
Both sides and the simulated fee are shown before agreement. Acceptance rechecks
ownership, account eligibility and lineup restrictions; both card movements and
simulated charges succeed together or none does. Saved unsettled teams lock their
cards against swaps; settled historical teams and earned points remain recorded.

in each 28-day server-timed cycle. The owner may exercise free/paid conditions for
one hour solely for testing. Such a change invalidates outstanding fee quotes and
requires a new offer. These tests do not approve a future commercial fee.

reserve cards come from finite isolated beta inventory and remain auditable.

Ordinary tester login uses email/password with verified invitation admission. Owner
and privileged operations require MFA. Authenticator setup keys are private and
used once during setup; later challenges use the changing six-digit code.

The counterparty can inspect eligible beta cards and shared trade provenance.
No password, factor secret, private account preference or administrator access is
shared. Beta records remain isolated from official assets and rankings. Support
handles disputes and account suspension. Provider data-location, retention/hold and
verified export/deletion obligations recorded in rc2 remain outstanding until their
specific acceptance evidence is completed. No claim of complete erasure or exclusive
Australian processing is made by this addendum.

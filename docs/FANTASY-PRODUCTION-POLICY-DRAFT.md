## Reward conditions

- **Starter:** one free pack of 11 CORE cards per eligible verified member account, claimed after the required onboarding/consent. The formation allocation is 1 goalkeeper, 4 defenders, 4 midfielders and 2 forwards, with unique players within the pack. Claims are permanently recorded and cannot be reset by retries or later inventory releases. Initial allocation is 1,000 Starter packs; additional bounded releases require an authorized MFA administrator and cannot increase permanent edition caps. If stock is unavailable, no successful claim is recorded. The launch homepage must disclose availability rather than promise unlimited immediate stock.
- **Daily:** claim 10 non-transferable gameplay points once in each UTC calendar day after claiming the Starter. The server displays the next claim time. Missed periods are not backdated. A repeated request returns the original result; it does not grant another reward. A local calendar day can differ from the UTC reward period.
- **Every seventh successful claim:** a controlled one-card CORE reward pack is available subject to remaining inventory and the shared daily card limit. These are seven successful claims, not necessarily seven consecutive days. Initial limits are 100 reward cards across all members per UTC day and 10,000 reward packs in the initial allocation. When card stock or the daily card limit is exhausted, the daily points still apply and the receipt records the reason no card pack was awarded. That receipt does not create an automatic later card entitlement.
- **Permanent records:** each claim records its applicable reward-policy version. The member view shows recent history; the database retains permanent claim and card-provenance records. Neither points nor card rarity changes fantasy performance scoring. No cash value is promised.
- **Changes:** reward configuration can be changed only through authorized MFA administration within the implemented bounds. The launch configuration remains 10 points and a seven-claim interval. Any future member-facing change must be communicated accurately and must not rewrite previous receipts or increase a launched edition's supply cap.

## Complaints and privacy requests — proposed operating procedure

Email **support@docked.com.au** with “Docked complaint”, “Moderation appeal” or “Privacy request”, a short description, relevant dates and the outcome sought. Provide your username or account email where needed, not passwords, verification links, payment information or unnecessary identity documents. A representative may be asked for appropriate authority. Docked may request proportionate information to verify an account-related request without routinely collecting government identity documents.

The proposed procedure is to acknowledge receipt, assign an authorized person, assess the relevant records and explain the outcome and available review options. We aim to respond within 30 calendar days; if more time is needed, explain why and provide an update. For a moderation appeal, use a different reviewer where practicable. Record only the information needed to handle the complaint and any justified follow-up. These commitments require an assigned owner and approval before publication.

Where the Privacy Act applies, a person who is dissatisfied with Docked's response, or has received no response after 30 days, may complain to the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints/lodge-a-privacy-complaint-with-us). This does not claim Docked belongs to an external dispute resolution scheme. Consumers may also seek advice from their state or territory consumer-protection authority. Applicable rights are not limited by this procedure.

## Privacy Policy

The operator above is responsible for Docked's handling of personal information. Docked uses account email and identifiers, username/profile, self-attested age and country/state, consent versions, preferences, authentication and security records. Passwords are submitted to the authentication service. The game stores card allocations and ownership history, pack claims, gameplay points, teams, competitions and results. Social features store submitted posts/comments/images, relationships, reactions, saves, reports, blocks, mutes and notifications. Do not submit identity documents, payment-card details, sportsbook credentials or precise GPS location; these are not needed for the approved launch.

Information is used to authenticate accounts, run the game, enforce scarcity and ownership, prevent abuse, moderate social features, respond to support requests and record consent. Profile/social visibility follows the product's access and blocking rules; private preferences, reports and saves are not public. Optional analytics and marketing consent remain separate from necessary account/security processing. Optional outbound marketing and Android push are not enabled by this release. Historical storefront mailing lists are not imported into Fantasy communications.

The intended production providers are Netlify for website delivery, Supabase for authentication/database services in Sydney, and Microsoft 365 for support and transactional email. Only necessary data should be sent to each provider. Supabase's current residency guidance describes regional database/Auth/storage and backup hosting, with some support, service metadata and delivery processing outside the region, including US/Singapore support and EU logs. Netlify operates global delivery infrastructure and lists US infrastructure subprocessors. Microsoft mailbox geography depends on the tenant's actual configuration, which still needs administrator verification. The launch policy must identify likely overseas recipients/countries where practicable; it must not promise that all data stays in Australia. Separate Preview resources are not used to hold production accounts.

Members can use the available account controls to change preferences, export data and request deletion. The operator must verify these flows against production before promising them publicly. Deletion must revoke access and remove or pseudonymise eligible profile/social data while preserving the minimum lawful audit and scarcity evidence. Pseudonymous identifiers may still be personal information; an immutable transaction requirement is not a blanket justification to retain all personal information forever. Final retention/destruction periods, backup expiry, exceptions/legal holds and account-to-ledger unlinking need documented review and implementation verification.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy concerns, contact support@docked.com.au using the procedure above. Requested access may need to be limited to protect another person's information or satisfy applicable law; explain a refusal and review options where required. Security incidents must be assessed promptly, contained and notified to affected people/regulators when applicable legal thresholds are met. The owner must assign responsibility for this process before launch.

## Retention disclosure and implementation review

The following is an evidence-based internal schedule for approval, not a claim that every automatic cleanup already runs in production. No retention period is presented as a universal legal requirement.

| Category | Existing implementation / proposed decision |
| --- | --- |
| Account and authentication | Access is revoked before Auth deletion and profile erasure; failed erasure is queued for bounded retries. Verify real production execution and operational retry ownership before promising completion times. Proposed target: complete eligible account erasure within 30 days of a verified request, subject to explained legal holds. |
| Social profile/content | Account disablement clears profile identity, removes post/comment bodies and media associations, and removes private social preferences/relationships through database triggers. Verify residual media/logs and worker execution in production. |
| Gameplay ownership and claims | Immutable records currently retain the original account UUID in membership, cards, packs, claims, entries and result history. They contain no email field, but are not necessarily anonymous. Preserve scarcity and provenance; obtain a documented necessity/retention assessment and define account-link minimization before public launch. Do not assert indefinite retention of all personal data is legally required. |
| Data access/export | Current account export covers account, consent and community data, but does not yet include a complete Fantasy historical export. Do not describe the self-service export as a complete copy of all gameplay data. Provide a verified support-assisted route until a scoped historical export is implemented and tested. |
| Temporary social data | Existing maintenance queries erase expired quarantined uploads, expired notifications and completed notification jobs older than 90 days. Their presence in source is not evidence that a production scheduler is running. |
| Support/complaints and sent Auth mail | Proposed review point: 12 months after the case closes for routine support, with longer documented retention only where necessary for disputes/legal obligations; promptly remove expired verification-message bodies where no longer needed. Microsoft 365 retention/hold rules need administrator confirmation before selecting final expiry values. |
| Security logs and backups | Verify actual provider expiry and restore behavior; do not promise immediate deletion from backups. Configure the shortest operationally adequate periods within existing plans, and ensure restoration does not restore deleted users' access. No paid retention add-on is approved. |
| Optional analytics/marketing | Keep marketing disabled and optional analytics consent separate. Existing account erasure deletes per-user analytics; no historical marketing lists are imported. Confirm any wider event-expiry schedule before stating a fixed duration. |

Before publishing the policy, approve the final schedule, assign the person responsible for requests/holds, and test the deletion/export path with controlled accounts. Record reasons and review dates for retained identifiers rather than using “immutable” as a blanket privacy exception.

The operating model

From request to live site, without the usual back-and-forth.

  1. 01

    Customer subscribes

    The customer shares the business name, preferred domain, style, pages, logo needs, and launch goals.

  2. 02

    Docked prepares a preview

    We check the domain, create a private website or logo preview, and send the customer a review link before payment.

  3. 03

    Customer asks

    Changes are handled in a simple back-and-forth loop until the customer is happy with the direction.

  4. 04

    Approve and activate

    After approval, Docked buys and manages the domain, sets up the monthly agreement, publishes the site, and keeps it active.

Warranty & support

We’ll help make it right.

If your Cruise D2 is faulty, damaged or not operating as expected, stop using it and contact Docked.

Your rights matter. Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing on this page limits any rights or remedies available to you under applicable law.

How to request support

Email support@docked.com.au and include:

  • Your name and order reference.
  • A clear description of the issue and when it began.
  • Photos or video showing the product and issue, where practical.
  • Any relevant messages, warning lights or unusual behaviour.

Before sending the product

Do not send a product to the correspondence address until Docked has reviewed your request and provided return instructions. Lithium-battery products can require specific transport arrangements.

Safety-related issues

Stop use immediately if the product, battery, charger or propulsion system is damaged, leaking, swollen, unusually hot or operating abnormally. Keep it away from water and combustible materials where it is safe to do so, and contact us.

Assessment and remedies

We may ask for reasonable information needed to assess the issue. Available remedies depend on the circumstances and your applicable consumer-law rights. A separate voluntary warranty period is not advertised on this website.
